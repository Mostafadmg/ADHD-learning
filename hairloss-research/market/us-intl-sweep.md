# Hair-loss market sweep: United States and rest of world

Checked on 23 November 2026. Companion to the UK sweep (`uk-sweep.md`). The same data, one object per company, is in `us-intl-sweep.json`.

## What this is

A wide look at who sells hair-loss treatment outside the UK, so that no important seller or idea is missed. It covers **57 sellers** in the United States, Canada, Australia, mainland Europe and Asia: online prescribers, compounders (pharmacies that mix a medicine to order), women's health clinics, the big shop brands, laser devices and two diagnostic tests.

Already covered in separate deep dives, so left out here: Hims, Hers, Keeps, Ro, Happy Head, Strut Health, Musely, XYON, Oana Health, Nutrafol (all US) and Pilot (Australia).

**How it was done.** Public pages only, read with a web fetcher, a headless browser and web search. No accounts were made and no contact details were entered. Every entry lists its source links. Where a price or ingredient could not be seen in public, the entry says so. Company claims (customer numbers, results) are marked as claims.

**Words used.** *Compounded* = mixed to order by a pharmacy and not a licensed product (in the UK this is called an unlicensed "special"). *Off-label* = a licensed medicine used for something its licence does not cover (for example oral minoxidil or dutasteride for hair). *DHT* = the hormone that shrinks hair follicles in pattern hair loss; finasteride and dutasteride lower it.

## Five things that changed the market

1. **Hims & Hers has bought its way into Europe and Australia.** It bought ZAVA (UK, Germany, France, Ireland; 1.3 million active customers) in July 2025 and Eucalyptus (Pilot, Juniper; revenue run-rate above US$450 million) in 2026 for up to US$1.15 billion. Pilot became Hims in August 2026 and Juniper became Hers in October 2026. Sources: [Hims & Hers on ZAVA](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2025/Hims--Hers-Announces-Plans-to-Acquire-ZAVA-Accelerating-Major-European-Growth-Across-the-UK-Germany-France-and-Ireland/default.aspx), [Fierce Healthcare on Eucalyptus](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/digital-health/hims-hers-eyes-international-growth-115b-eucalyptus-acquisition), [Hers Australia launch](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2026/Hers-Launches-in-Australia-Built-to-Care-for-the-Whole-Woman/default.aspx).
2. **Plain generics are now almost free in the US.** Amazon sells a visit from US$29 and finasteride by post; GoodRx bundles visit, tablets and delivery from US$16 a month; Costco's own minoxidil costs under US$3 a month. Paid brands must now win on combinations, service and guarantees. Sources: [Amazon One Medical](https://www.onemedical.com/amazon/pay-per-visit/), [GoodRx release](https://investors.goodrx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/goodrx-launches-new-hair-loss-subscription-men-simplify-access), [Costco deal post](https://slickdeals.net/f/19019587-today-only-12-25-2025-minoxidil-sale-at-costco-com-topical-solution-13-99-5-99-or-foam-39-99-5-99-shipping).
3. **Regulators are watching compounded topical finasteride.** In April 2025 the US FDA warned about side-effect reports (32 cases, 2019 to 2024, some lasting after stopping) and reminded people that no topical finasteride is FDA-approved. In Germany, a court case over MySpring's batch-made finasteride and minoxidil solution reached the Federal Court of Justice in February 2026. Sources: [FDA alert](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fda-alerts-health-care-providers-compounders-and-consumers-potential-risks-associated-compounded), [Apotheke Adhoc](https://www.apotheke-adhoc.de/nachrichten/detail/markt/myspring-defektur-trick-und-rx-werbung-wellster-uebernimmt-versandapotheke/).
4. **Women are served through menopause and women's health brands** (Alloy, Midi, Nurx, Wisp), with oral minoxidil and spironolactone as the standard pair.
5. **New licensed medicines are close.** An extended-release minoxidil tablet and a new anti-androgen scalp solution have both passed Phase 3 trials (see section 11).

#### Ulo

A newer hair-only telehealth compounder, co-founded by the hair-loss education site Perfect Hair Health, built around a "Treatment Customizer" with unusual actives, strength choices and delivery forms.

- **Country:** United States
- **Who it serves:** Men and women (age bands 19-49 and 50+); prescriptions in all 50 US states; non-prescription products shipped worldwide.
- **Products:**
  - **0.005% Topical Finasteride (6-in-1), men**: Finasteride 0.005% (a deliberate micro-dose) + minoxidil 7% + tretinoin + melatonin 0.01% + caffeine 0.2% + cetirizine 1%; a 0.2% full-strength option also exists. Price: From US$42 a month (longest plan).
  - **0.2% Topical Dutasteride (6-in-1), men**: Dutasteride 0.2% + minoxidil 7% + tretinoin 0.01% + melatonin 0.01% + caffeine 0.2% + cetirizine 1%. Price: From US$61 a month.
  - **7% Topical Minoxidil (5-in-1)**: Minoxidil 7% + tretinoin + melatonin + caffeine + cetirizine (no DHT blocker). Price: From US$35 a month (men); from US$32 (women).
  - **Oral options, men**: Dutasteride 0.5 mg; finasteride 1 mg; minoxidil 2.5 mg. Price: From US$29, US$18 and US$22 a month.
  - **Sublingual Minoxidil, women**: Minoxidil 2.25 mg under-the-tongue + folic acid 0.4 mg + vitamin D 1000 IU. Price: From US$42 a month.
  - **Oral Spironolactone combination, women**: Spironolactone 50 mg + minoxidil 0.625 mg + folic acid 1 mg + vitamin D 1000 IU (also spironolactone 200 mg alone; oral minoxidil 1.25 mg alone). Price: From US$59 a month (200 mg alone from US$44; minoxidil 1.25 mg from US$18).
  - **0.2% Topical Finasteride, postmenopausal women**: Finasteride 0.2% topical. Price: From US$38 a month.
- **Compounded:** Yes
- **Consultation:** 2-minute hair quiz matches a protocol; after purchase a short medical intake is reviewed by a licensed provider. Subscriptions include on-demand access to hair specialists and doctors.
- **Distinctive:** The most adventurous menu found: sublingual minoxidil, topical cetirizine (an antihistamine studied for hair), melatonin and caffeine boosters, micro-dose (0.005%) versus full-strength (0.2%) topical finasteride, topical dutasteride, 7% minoxidil, and no propylene glycol in any formula. The customer builds the treatment: delivery form, DHT reducer, growth stimulant, add-ons. 12-month (365-day) money-back guarantee.
- **Size signal:** Small and young: about 65-70 Trustpilot reviews (mixed). No customer numbers published. The most detailed review is by its own co-founder (conflict of interest).
- **Deserves a deep dive:** Yes
- **Sources:** [1](https://ulo.co/) · [2](https://perfecthairhealth.com/ulo-review/) · [3](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/ulo.co)

#### Miiskin

A dermatology app and platform that connects patients with independent US board-certified dermatologists, who can prescribe a wide menu of compounded hair formulas from partner pharmacies.

- **Country:** United States
- **Who it serves:** Men and women; state coverage depends on the dermatologist.
- **Products:**
  - **Single-ingredient compounded items**: Minoxidil, finasteride or dutasteride (topical or oral, via partner pharmacy). Price: US$24.99 each.
  - **Compounded combinations**: For example minoxidil + finasteride; minoxidil + dutasteride; combinations including latanoprost. Price: US$48.99 (minoxidil + finasteride) up to US$68.99 (combinations with latanoprost).
  - **Dermatologist consultation**: Photo-based, asynchronous. Price: US$59 first consult; about US$30-49 follow-up (pages differ).
- **Compounded:** Yes
- **Consultation:** Photo-based consultation in an app with a named board-certified dermatologist; reply in 1 to 2 working days. No subscription; the patient can choose their pharmacy.
- **Distinctive:** Real dermatologists and the widest public menu of unusual actives at fixed prices: topical dutasteride and latanoprost.
- **Size signal:** Small in hair. No customer numbers published.
- **Deserves a deep dive:** No

## 10. The most innovative ideas that UK companies do not offer yet

Each idea was checked against the UK sweep (`uk-sweep.json`, 58 UK sellers) and the UK deep-dive notes in this folder. "Not found in the UK" means not found in that research; it is not proof that nobody in the UK does it.

### New actives and delivery forms

1. **Sublingual minoxidil (a tablet that melts under the tongue).** Ulo sells 2.25 mg with folic acid and vitamin D for women, from US$42 a month. Samson Clinical in Australia finished enrolling a Phase 3 trial in men (2.5 mg twice a day) in November 2025; results were not found. Not found in the UK. Sources: [Ulo](https://ulo.co/), [Clinical Trials Arena](https://www.clinicaltrialsarena.com/news/samson-clinical-concludes-phaseiii-trial/), [early study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34420241/).
2. **Latanoprost in the scalp mix.** Jack Health's "Hello Hair" is minoxidil 5% + finasteride 0.1% + latanoprost 0.03%. Miiskin's partner pharmacy sells latanoprost combinations at US$68.99. Not found in the UK. Sources: [Jack Health](https://jack.health/finasteride-vs-minoxidil-vs-hello-hair-solutions), [Miiskin price list](https://miiskin.com/foothills-professional-pharmacy/compounded-prescriptions-hair/).
3. **Topical cetirizine as a booster.** Ulo adds cetirizine 1% (an antihistamine) with melatonin and caffeine to every topical. Not found in the UK. Source: [Ulo](https://ulo.co/).
4. **A licensed finasteride scalp spray.** Fynzur (finasteride 2.275 mg/ml) is a licensed medicine in Germany and many other EU countries; ZAVA Germany sells it from EUR 75.32. UK sellers offer only unlicensed compounded sprays (one UK seller, UsMen, says plainly that no licensed topical finasteride exists in the UK). Sources: [ZAVA Germany](https://www.zavamed.com/de/haarausfall.html), [German leaflet listing](https://www.patienteninfo-service.de/a-z-liste/f/fynzur-fuer-maenner-2275-mgml-spray-zur-anwendung-auf-der-haut-kopfhaut-loesung), [trial paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9297965).
5. **A combined capsule for women.** Ulo: spironolactone 50 mg + minoxidil 0.625 mg + folic acid + vitamin D in one capsule (from US$59 a month). Alloy: oral minoxidil with vitamin D. In the UK, combined capsules were found only for men. Sources: [Ulo](https://ulo.co/), [Alloy](https://www.myalloy.com/solutions/oral-minoxidil-1).
6. **Chewable dutasteride with minoxidil.** Keeps sells a prescription chew combining the two (about US$59.99 a month, per Fortune). Hims UK already sells a finasteride and minoxidil chew, so the form exists in the UK, but not with dutasteride. Source: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/best-hair-loss-treatments/).
7. **A personalised single pill as a whole service.** The Hairy Pill (A$75 a month) puts everything in one daily capsule and reviews it by phone. UK clinics sell combined capsules, but no UK brand was found that runs it as a mass service with scheduled doctor reviews. Source: [The Hairy Pill](https://www.thehairypill.com.au/).

### Tests and tracking

8. **A "will minoxidil work for me?" test.** Daniel Alain's Minoxidil Response Test (US$139) measures the follicle enzyme that switches minoxidil on, from plucked hairs. The evidence comes mainly from the company's own scientists. Not found in the UK (the UK has Fagron's DNA TrichoTest through clinics instead). Sources: [Daniel Alain](https://blog.danielalain.com/articles/comparing-the-cost-of-different-hair-loss-tests), [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health-news/this-mail-in-test-may-show-if-minoxidil-will-help-your-hair-loss).
9. **A root-cause test, a coach and a diet plan.** Traya's Hair Test assigns causes (genetics, stress, gut, nutrition, dandruff), then a named coach, a diet plan and an app keep the customer going. Not found in the UK. Source: [Traya](https://traya.health/).
10. **AI reading of scalp photos at sign-up and over time.** MDhair gives a free AI assessment from a scalp photo and tracks progress; REVIAN's app logs every session and keeps a photo journal; stand-alone apps such as HairKit score density from phone photos. The UK sweep found photo tracking (Belgravia) but no AI scoring. Sources: [MDhair](https://www.mdhair.co/), [REVIAN](https://revian.com/), [HairKit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6761551603).

In the UK, hair blood tests exist but are sold separately (for example Superdrug at GBP 99). Sources: [MediaBrief](https://mediabrief.com/man-matters-introduces-at-home-blood-testing-for-hair-health/), [Superdrug](https://healthclinics.superdrug.com/blood-testing-hair-health/).

UK brands offer money-back guarantees, but none framed around side effects was found. Sources: [The Hairy Pill](https://www.thehairypill.com.au/), [Mosh](https://www.getmosh.com.au/hair-loss).

Not found in the UK. Sources: [Alloy](https://www.myalloy.com/hair-lash-brow-care), [Midi](https://www.joinmidi.com/hair-skin).

This builds trust and cuts refunds. Sources: [Nurx](https://www.nurx.com/hair-loss/), [Hårklinikken](https://harklinikken.com/products/extract), [Theradome](https://theradome.com/).

Sources: [Noah](https://www.ofnoah.sg/physical-health/hair-loss), [hub.health](https://hub.health/treatment/hair-loss/), [Gro Clinics](https://groclinics.com.au/pages/prescription-haircare), [Charles](https://www.charles.co/consulter-cheveux/), [BosleyRx](https://www.bosleyrx.com/products/finasteride).

Sources: [The Hairy Pill](https://www.thehairypill.com.au/), [Traya](https://traya.health/), [JMIR Dermatology](https://derma.jmir.org/2026/1/e84676).

## 12. Limits of this sweep

- **Sites that could not be read.** minoxidilmax.com did not resolve from the research environment (it would need to be allowed in the session's network settings); GoodRx, Cost Plus Drugs and Business Wire blocked automated reading. Those entries rely on press releases, search summaries and third-party listings, and say so.
- **Hidden prices and ingredients.** Canada, Australia and France restrict advertising of prescription medicines, so Felix, Jack Health, Phoenix, Mosh, hub.health, The Hairy Pill, Gro Clinics and Charles do not publish drug names or full prices. Finding them needs a consultation walk-through (a deep dive).
- **Not covered.** Latin America (for example Choiz in Mexico and Manual in Brazil) and South Korea were searched briefly but nothing could be verified, so they are left out. No large Nordic prescription telehealth brand was found; that may be a real gap in the market or a gap in the search.
- **Company claims.** Customer numbers and success rates are the companies' own figures unless a filing or news source is named.
- **Prices move.** Many prices were captured during Black Friday promotions (late November 2026). List prices are given where the page showed them.

## 13. Summary in ten lines

1. This sweep adds 57 sellers outside the UK to the 11 already in deep dives. The main online prescribers in the US, Canada, Australia and Germany are covered; the known gaps are listed in section 12.
2. The US mass market has split in two: near-free generics (Amazon from US$29 a visit, GoodRx from US$16 a month, Costco minoxidil under US$3 a month) and paid brands selling compounded combinations and service.
3. Hims & Hers now owns ZAVA in Europe and Pilot and Juniper in Australia, so one company's formulas and funnel are arriving in most English-speaking markets.
4. The most inventive seller is the small US compounder Ulo: sublingual minoxidil, topical cetirizine, micro-dose topical finasteride, topical dutasteride, a women's combination capsule and a 12-month guarantee.
5. Australia has the best service ideas: The Hairy Pill (one personalised pill, phone reviews, a side-effect promise) and Mosh (180-day, reformulation and price-match guarantees).
6. Women are served mostly by menopause and women's health brands (Alloy, Midi, Nurx, Wisp) using oral minoxidil plus spironolactone; this is the clearest model for the UK's women's gap.
7. Outside the West, Traya in India shows that a root-cause test plus a human coach and an app can build a hair-only brand with roughly GBP 30 million of yearly revenue.
8. Seven companies deserve a full deep dive: Ulo, The Hairy Pill, Mosh and Moshy, Traya, Curology, Alloy and Hårklinikken.
9. Ideas not found in the UK: sublingual minoxidil, latanoprost and cetirizine topicals, a licensed finasteride spray, a women's combined capsule, a minoxidil response test, coaching and AI photo tracking, side-effect guarantees and menopause-led hair care.
10. Two licensed medicines are close (extended-release oral minoxidil and clascoterone 5% solution); regulators in the US and Germany are also pressing on compounded topical finasteride, so any new UK service should be built to switch to licensed products when they arrive.
