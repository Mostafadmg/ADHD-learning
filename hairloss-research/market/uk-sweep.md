# UK hair-loss market sweep: everyone outside the ten deep dives

Checked on 23 November 2026. Companion data file: `uk-sweep.json` (58 entries, same facts, same sources).

**What this is.** A sweep of UK sellers of hair-loss treatment other than the ten already being studied in depth (Hims UK, Voy, Hair + Me, Sons, Numan, Treated, Oxford Online Pharmacy, Superdrug Online Doctor, Boots Online Doctor, LloydsPharmacy Online Doctor). The aim is to make sure nothing significant is missed, and to say which of the others deserve a full deep dive.

**How to read it.** Every fact comes from a public page read on the date above, and each provider lists its sources. Prices are the lowest shown on the page for the pack named; they change often. "Compounded" means the product is mixed to order or made as a "special". Such products are unlicensed medicines: they have not been through the medicines licensing process (Dr Fox and UsMen say this plainly on their own pages: [Dr Fox](https://www.doctorfox.co.uk/hair-loss-treatments/hair-growth-spray.html), [UsMen](https://usmen.co.uk/treatment-info/topical-minoxidil-finasteride-spray-for-hair-loss-treatment/)). "Off-label" means a licensed medicine used for something its licence does not cover, such as minoxidil tablets or dutasteride for hair loss.

**Limits.** No accounts were created and no consultation was submitted, so consultation detail is what each site says about itself. Five sites blocked automated reading (ZAVA, Asda Online Doctor, Simple Online Pharmacy, Pharmacy2U, HealthExpress); their prices come from search-result snippets of their own pages and are marked as such. No web-traffic tool was available in this session, so size is judged from review counts, customer claims, funding and ownership.

**6. Honesty about unlicensed status varies.** Dr Fox, UsMen, City Skin Clinic and Philip Kingsley's patient leaflet say in plain words that the compounded product is unlicensed. e-Surgery says "compounded" but not "unlicensed". Harley Street Hair Transplant Clinics does not say either. DrHair labels a product "unlicensed" while its subscription box on the same page says "UK licensed treatment". Sources are in each entry.

**7. Sons has become the pharmacies' house brand for minoxidil.** Sons minoxidil 5% is sold by Dr Fox, Chemist4U, Pharmacy2U, The Independent Pharmacy, Click2Pharmacy, Click Pharmacy, myPharmacy and Totiva (see each entry). That is useful context for the Sons deep dive.

**8. Market context to pass to the regulation team.**

- One UK pharmacy guide says a licensed finasteride spray (Finjuve) "has recently received UK marketing authorisation" ([Bolt Pharmacy, updated 17 Oct 2026](https://www.boltpharmacy.co.uk/guide/does-finasteride-spray-cause-erectile-dysfunction)). This could not be confirmed: a search of the medicines compendium for finasteride cutaneous spray returned nothing on 23 November 2026 ([emc search](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/search?q=finasteride+cutaneous+spray)), and UsMen and Totiva still say no licensed topical finasteride exists. **Unverified; needs checking with the MHRA.**
- The MHRA strengthened its warnings on finasteride and dutasteride on 11 May 2026 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/mhra-strengthens-safety-warnings-for-finasteride-and-dutasteride)).

## 7. Ten-line summary

1. 58 sellers were catalogued outside the ten existing deep dives: 49 prescribing services and clinics, and 9 non-prescription brands.
2. Five deserve a full deep dive: Dr Fox, Philip Kingsley (PK Lab), The Belgravia Centre, Dermatica and City Skin Clinic.
3. Dr Fox sets the price floor: finasteride at £10 a month and an unlicensed triple spray at £13 a month, with no subscription.
4. Philip Kingsley and Belgravia show the high-touch model: assessment, then subscription, video or clinic, with specially prepared formulas and, at Belgravia, no public price list.
5. Dermatica (women, from February 2026) and City Skin Clinic (bespoke serum, men and women) are the closest rivals to Hair + Me; confirm Dermatica is still taking new hair-loss customers.
6. The big pharmacies (Pharmacy2U, ZAVA and Asda, Chemist4U, MedExpress, Pharmica, Simple Online Pharmacy) are large but sell the same tablets and Regaine through a two-minute form; no deep dive needed.
7. Hims & Hers announced in June 2025 that it was buying ZAVA, which also runs Asda Online Doctor, so the Hims deep dive should treat them as one group.
8. Compounded finasteride-plus-minoxidil sprays are now sold by at least ten sellers outside the deep-dive list, from £13 to £40 a month, so the spray alone no longer sets a brand apart.
9. Women are under-served: nearly every pharmacy offers them only Regaine and supplements, and the pharmacies sell off-label tablets to men only.
10. Open questions: whether a licensed finasteride spray (Finjuve) now exists in the UK, and live prices for the five sites that blocked reading.
