# Products: the full range

Part of the final blueprint (see `plan.md` for the summary, the evidence marks and the labels). Every price is a
**[proposal]** and every cost is an **[assumption]** until two wholesalers have quoted. Doses come from the
fact-checked clinical files; none is final until NICE CKS, the BNF, UKTIS and the full oral minoxidil consensus paper
have been read (`risks.md` 3.7).

Evidence marks on rivals: **\*** not walked live (Voy, Hers US, Oxford Online Pharmacy); **†** final review not
finished (Sons, Ro); **‡** not verified (XYON). Nothing after any rival's sign-in wall was watched.

**Internal document: never publish any part of it as it stands.** [reviewed: corrected] This file names
prescription-only medicines (POMs) next to prices, effect figures and "Beats" lines that compare us with named rivals.
Published, that would be an advert likely to lead to the use of a POM (Human Medicines Regulations 2012, regulation
284), a claim that effects are "better than" another identifiable treatment (regulation 287(1); CAP Code 12.19), and,
for M6 and S2, an advert for an unlicensed medicine (regulation 279). Public pages may describe the condition and the
service only, as `launch.md` and the copy check set out [uk-regulation 2.1, 2.2]. Off-label uses (for example scalp
minoxidil for traction or after childbirth) must not be suggested on any public page either, because an advert must
match the product's SmPC (regulation 280).

## 1. Price and plan rules [proposal]

1. **One price per calendar month, whatever the delivery rhythm.** Monthly parcels or one parcel every 3 months cost
   the same per month. Billing is monthly [VT 3.2].
2. **All-in:** prescriber, every review, photo checks, messages and tracked delivery. No consultation, prescription
   or delivery fees.
3. **No discounts of any kind:** no first-order price, no retention offer, no cheaper plan for paying ahead. The MHRA
   says special offers on medicine prices should not be highlighted, and the GPhC treated a first-month discount on a
   prescription medicine as an improper incentive at Voy [uk-regulation 2.2, 3.5]. CP's optional 10% 3-monthly price
   is dropped [judging 4.4].
4. **The first two supplies are one month each**, so the plan can change after the 2 to 4 week check without wasting
   medicine. Then monthly or every 3 months, the patient's choice.
5. **Same price for each base.** Men choose liquid or foam; women choose 5% foam or 2% solution. Feel, not price,
   decides.
6. **Price changes:** reviewed every 6 months under the price floor rule (`launch.md` 5.6), with 30 days' written notice
   and the right to leave first. No 12-month price lock: VT's lock left no room to correct [judging 3.3].
7. **Trust line inside the consultation (result screen and written plan), not on public product pages or adverts:**
   "Minoxidil 5% is on general sale. You can buy it in a shop without us. People choose us for the assessment, the
   follow-up and the photo reviews." It must stay factual and must not compare effects with other products [VT 3.4].
   [reviewed: corrected] A public page that names minoxidil is an advert for a medicine, and a medicine advert may not
   offer "a diagnosis" or suggest a treatment online (regulation 286; CAP Code 12.2.1) [uk-regulation 2.1, 2.4]. So
   the line lives only where the person has already started a consultation, and "diagnosis" is replaced by "assessment".

## 3. How we make products better

### 3.1 Six formulation rules [CP 3.1]

1. **Separate medicines, not fixed mixes.** The MHRA says finasteride 1 mg must be stopped at once if depression or
   suicidal thoughts appear. A combined pill or an all-in-one lotion forces the patient to stop everything. No trial of
   a fixed combination pill was found [clinical-men 9].
2. **Strengths that match the trials.** Scalp minoxidil 5% at most (the only trial of 10% found it worse than 5% and more
   irritating [clinical-men 3.1]). Oral minoxidil at consensus doses from a licensed tablet.
3. **The base chosen for the person.** Solution (propylene glycol and ethanol) or foam (no propylene glycol). Propylene
   glycol is the usual cause of so-called minoxidil "allergy" [clinical-men 3.1]. An itch means a base switch, not a
   ban.
4. **Pairs only where trials show added benefit.** One DHT-lowering drug (finasteride or dutasteride) with one growth
   drug (minoxidil). Never two 5-ARIs, never oral plus scalp minoxidil, never oral plus scalp finasteride
   [clinical-men 9]. (*DHT* is the hormone that shrinks sensitive follicles; a *5-ARI* lowers it.)
5. **No passengers.** No biotin (it can falsify blood tests, including troponin, a heart-attack test), no tretinoin,
   cetirizine, latanoprost, melatonin, caffeine or steroid inside a prescription product [clinical-men 3.4, 4, 6, 9].
6. **Specials to a known standard.** Bought from an MHRA-licensed specials manufacturer with stability data. We do
   **not** make finasteride specials ourselves: finasteride powder must not be handled by pregnant women
   [uk-regulation 3.3; judging 3.3 item 8].

### 3.2 Product-feel rules (about 5% of negative reviews are greasy or smelly products, 7% irritation, 7% faulty sprays [customer-voice 3.5]) [VT 3.7]

1. Choose liquid or foam (men), or 5% foam or 2% solution (women), **at the same price**.
2. **One swap of base or form in the first 3 months at no extra cost** (the month is already paid), if clinically
   suitable and after the checks. [reviewed: corrected] Never described or advertised as "free": free offers are not
   permitted for any medicine (MHRA Blue Guide Appendix 6) and promotional free supply is banned (regulation 293)
   [uk-regulation 2.1, 2.2].
3. **Ask about routine first:** how often you wash, styling products, whether residue is a deal-breaker, textured hair.
   Residue and styling problems are a recognised reason to choose tablets [clinical-men 3.2].
4. **Licensed packs with long shelf lives** (Regaine 5% solution: 36 months [clinical-men 3.1]) instead of compounded
   bottles that expire in a month.
5. **Faulty pump or can:** replaced at no extra cost within 2 working days.
6. **Pet and family safety built in:** a cat at home triggers handling advice and an honest look at tablets.
   [reviewed: corrected] Minoxidil tablets are off-label, so a pet in the home is only a reason for them when the
   prescriber records why they serve this person better than careful use of the licensed scalp product (MHRA
   prescribers' responsibilities) [uk-regulation 5.1].
7. **An application card and a 60-second how-to video in every first parcel:** dry scalp, how much, how often, wash
   hands, what itch means.
8. **Itch on a liquid?** Switch to the foam. Only a true minoxidil allergy removes all minoxidil.
