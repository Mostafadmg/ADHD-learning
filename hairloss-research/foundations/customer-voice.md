# Customer voice: what people love and hate about online hair-loss companies

Status: DRAFT IN PROGRESS (research started 23 Nov 2026, continued 23 Nov 2026). Sections are filled in as sources are read.

Reader: a UK pharmacist independent prescriber planning an online hair-loss service for men and women.

Goal: a ranked list of unmet needs that a new UK competitor could win on.

**Rules used in this file**

- Every fact carries a source link. Anything that could not be checked is marked **[unverified]** or left out.
- Review examples are paraphrased (reworded), never quoted in full, and carry no usernames.
- No review or number is invented. Where a site blocked the research machine, this is stated.
- "What a better service would do" lines are the author's suggestions, built from the sourced complaints.

### 1.2 How the Trustpilot reviews were counted

- For each company, the most recent 1-star and 2-star reviews (up to 200 per star level, the logged-out limit), recent 3-star reviews and about 120 recent 4–5-star reviews were saved.
- For companies that sell many things (Hims, Hers, Numan, Voy, Ro, Musely, the high-street online doctors), only reviews that mention hair, minoxidil, finasteride, the scalp and similar words were kept. Weight-loss reviews were removed.
- Each review was screened with keyword patterns for each theme (for example "cancel", "unsubscribe", "still charged" for the cancellation theme). One review can count under several themes. **These are keyword screens, not a hand-coded study**: they over-count some themes (for example "delivery" also catches "delivered on time") and under-count others. Use the percentages to compare companies and to rank themes roughly, not as exact rates.
- Review links use Trustpilot's standard review address, `https://uk.trustpilot.com/reviews/<review id>`, built from the review id in the page data.

What this shows:

- **UK specialists sit at about 4.2–4.3 with 11–16% of reviews at 1–2 stars.** US hair brands are much worse on Trustpilot: Hims US 2.9, Hers US 3.3, Keeps 3.2, Nutrafol 2.5.
- **The high-street online doctors get almost no hair-loss reviews.** Hair is a tiny part of their business, so their scores say little about their hair service.
- **Treated, Strut Health (struthealth.com) and Hims's own hims.com page** had no usable Trustpilot hair sample (pages missing or almost empty).
- **Many 4–5-star reviews are "invited"** (the company asked the customer to review, often soon after the first order). At Hair + Me 118 of 120 recent positive reviews were invited, and at Hims US 87 of 98. Positive reviews therefore often describe sign-up and delivery, not results.

### 1.4 Regulator and press findings already checked

- **ASA, 8 Feb 2023, Hims & Hers UK (upheld).** The website promoted prescription-only finasteride tablets and unlicensed compounded products (topical finasteride with minoxidil) to the public. The ASA noted that "unlicensed medicines had not been tested for efficacy, quality or safety". [ASA ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/hims---hers-uk-ltd-a22-1145016-hims--hers-uk-ltd-1.html)
- **ASA, 5 Oct 2022 and 6 Mar 2024, Numan (both upheld).** TV adverts for Regaine with a "see results in 180 days or your money back" offer were judged to imply that the medicine was guaranteed to work. [2022 ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/vir-health-ltd-a22-1154781-vir-health-ltd.html), [2024 ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/vir-health-ltd-a22-1178070-vir-health-ltd.html)
- **ASA, 4 Jun 2025, Menwell Ltd t/a Manual (upheld).** A Google advert said "Buy Oral Minoxidil - £14/Month ... Effective in 9/10 Men". Oral minoxidil is prescription-only and licensed only for some types of high blood pressure. [ASA ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/menwell-ltd-a25-1277445-menwell-ltd.html)
- **MHRA, Sept 2021, Sons.** A member of the public complained that mental-health side effects of finasteride were not properly discussed on the Sons website; the MHRA asked Sons to give a balanced view of risks and "avoid absolute statements of safety". [MHRA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/advertising-investigations-september-2021/september-2021-promotion-of-finasteride-by-careforsons-limited-ta-sons)
- **BBC News, 24 Jan 2025.** "Some online sites are prescribing men a hair loss drug that has potentially risky side effects without consistent safety checks." A man who bought finasteride after a "tick-box" form said he had "no consultation with a doctor. No zoom meeting", and sent no photos. In the BBC's test purchases from Hims, Superdrug and Boots, only Boots asked for a photo, Superdrug charged extra to chat with a doctor, and no pack contained the new patient alert card. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05p1pnvymvo)
- **BBC Radio 4, File on 4 Investigates, 20 Jan 2026.** The programme reported that "some hair loss clinics are failing to adequately warn customers at the consultation stage" about sexual side effects, and found TikTok influencer accounts promoting finasteride with discount codes for Manual (Manual said it was not involved in making the videos). [BBC Sounds](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sounds/play/m002q2k1)
