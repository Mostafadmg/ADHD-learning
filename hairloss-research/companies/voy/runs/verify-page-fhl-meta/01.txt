Will it work for me?

Hair loss has different causes. Voy has treatments built for all of them.

Hormonal hair loss

Natural shifts in hormones can shorten your hair’s growth phase, so it sheds more and grows back finer. Our Minoxidil based treatments address exactly that by keeping your hair in growth phase longer.

Menopause

Falling oestrogen can thin hair along the parting and crown. It’s common, and it’s treatable. Your clinician checks your full medical history before recommending anything.

Postpartum

Shedding after birth is driven by the hormone drop. For many women it settles, and if it hasn’t, or you want support, we can help.

Stress or illness

Surgery, illness or a very hard year can push hair into shedding two to three months later. It’s often reversible once the cause is addressed and can be reset by treatment that prolongs your hair’s growth phase.

Faq

Questions women ask
How strong is the treatment?

Your clinician sets the strength based on your answers and medical history. You’ll see it before you confirm, and it can be changed later.

I’m already using a hair loss treatment.

Tell us in your consultation. Your clinician will take it into account and explain how your plan compares.

What’s in it? I have allergies.

You’ll see the full ingredient list before you pay, and we’ll remove any active you have allergies to.

Will I shed more at the start?

Some women do in the first weeks. It’s usually temporary and a sign the cycle is resetting. Your clinician will talk you through it.

What happens if I stop?

Results last while you continue treatment. If you stop, hair gradually returns to its previous path. You choose when to stop, and your clinician can help advise what to do for maintenance.

Is minoxidil toxic to pets, and how can I use it safely around cats and dogs?

Yes, even tiny amounts of minoxidil are toxic to cats and dogs. To use it safely:

Oral: Store in a locked or high cabinet out of reach.
Topical: Wash hands immediately after applying, keep pets away during application, clean spills, cover pillowcases, and dispose of containers in a secure bin. Avoid pet contact with treated areas.
Are there side effects?

All effective treatment can have them. Most are minor, and you’ll see the full list for your plan before you pay.
