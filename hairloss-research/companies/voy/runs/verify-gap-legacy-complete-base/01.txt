How long will it take to see results?

Finasteride and minoxidil best results are seen at 12 months, with some early changes at 4-6 months. The first 3 months will typically be a period of shedding of the old hair, and the start of a new hair growth cycle. This is normal, and a sign the treatment is working.

What kind of hair loss does this treat?

Minoxidil and Finasteride are clinically proven to be 94% effective when used together at improving all types of hair loss including:
●  Receding hairlines
●  Thinning at the crown
●  Thinning all over

Combined treatment, targeting the root causes
Dr. Earim Chaudry, Medical Director
Finasteride

Finasteride blocks the enzyme that produces DHT. This prevents hair loss and promotes hair regrowth.

Minoxidil

Minoxidil acts as a hair fertiliser, increasing blood flow to follicles to promote longer, thicker hair growth.

Why we lose hair

DHT (a male hormone) shrinks your hair follicles causing hair thinning, and eventually baldness.
