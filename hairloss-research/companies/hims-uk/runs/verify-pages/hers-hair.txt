Kickstart growth

Active ingredients start working from day one, boosting blood flow to the follicles to help reduce shedding and extend the active growth phase.

Extend the growth period

The growth phase of the hair cycle extends while the resting phase shortens, promoting visibly thicker hair and noticeably less shedding.

Thicken and maintain

See continued regrowth and stronger strands. Treatment continues to manage the root causes of hair thinning to help you maintain long-term results.
