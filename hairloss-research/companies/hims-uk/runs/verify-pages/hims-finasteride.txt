How to take finasteride tablets


Take one tablet once daily with or without food.

Consistency is key. If you miss a day, it’s all good, just skip it and get back on your game the next day. Just don’t take two doses to make up for it.

If you miss a dose, it’s all good, take a dose as soon as you remember. If it is almost time for your next dose, wait until then and take a regular dose.  Just don’t take two doses to make up for it.
