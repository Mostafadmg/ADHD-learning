Frequently asked questions

What is Drop+ 11-In-1?

Drop+ is a compounded topical treatment that combines dutasteride, minoxidil, and 9 supporting ingredients into a single daily scalp application for comprehensive hair loss treatment.

How does dutasteride differ from finasteride?

Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 forms of the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT, while finasteride blocks only type 2. This means dutasteride can more comprehensively reduce DHT levels.

How long does it take to see results?

Most people begin to see changes after 4-6 months of consistent daily use. Continued use is recommended to maintain results.
