ABOUT THIS TREATMENT

Gel+ combines ingredients that address male pattern hair loss through several complementary pathways. Minoxidil stimulates hair follicles, while dutasteride and finasteride reduce the effects of DHT, the hormone that contributes to progressive follicle miniaturization in male pattern hair loss. Latanoprost supports the hair-growth cycle, while the additional ingredients provide support for follicular activity, minoxidil response, and scalp and hair health.

 Gel+ is a compounded medication that is not FDA-approved. Its combination of ingredients has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or effectiveness, and some ingredients are used off-label to treat hair loss.

Frequently asked questions

What is Gel+?

Keeps Gel+ is a unique 10-in-1 compounded topical formula designed to combat hair loss and promote healthy hair growth through multiple pathways. It combines dutasteride, finasteride, minoxidil, and latanoprost with tretinoin, cetirizine, melatonin, caffeine, vitamin D3, and biotin in one daily scalp gel.

How is Gel+ different from Chew+?

Chew+ is a 5-in-1 chewable tablet you take by mouth, combining dutasteride, minoxidil, biotin, vitamin C, and zinc. Gel+ is a 10-in-1 gel you apply to your scalp, combining dutasteride, finasteride, minoxidil, and latanoprost with six supporting ingredients.

What's the difference between dutasteride and finasteride?

Both are DHT blockers used to treat male pattern hair loss. Finasteride blocks one type of the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT. Dutasteride blocks multiple types, making it a more comprehensive DHT blocker. Dutasteride is typically recommended for progressive hair loss where finasteride alone may not be sufficient.

Is Gel+ safe? What are the side effects?

The most common side effects are related to the scalp and may include dryness, scaling, itching, redness, burning, irritation, or allergic reactions. Unwanted facial or body hair can occur if the medication is accidentally transferred outside the treatment area.

While oral dutasteride can cause systemic side effects, including sexual dysfunction and mood changes such as depression or suicidal thoughts, limited early studies suggest topical dutasteride may have a lower risk of systemic side effects than oral treatment. However, the safety and systemic absorption of this specific compounded Gel+ formulation have not been established. Although uncommon, topical medications can be absorbed through the skin. Stop using Gel+ and contact the clinical team if you develop significant scalp irritation, swelling, blistering or rash; dizziness or fainting; palpitations; chest pain; shortness of breath; rapid weight gain; swelling of the legs or face; new sexual symptoms; breast tenderness or enlargement; or new mood symptoms such as depression, suicidal thoughts, or anxiety.

Gel+ should not be used by patients with a history of heart disease, other than high blood pressure. Do not donate blood while using Gel+ and for at least 6 months after stopping treatment. Avoid applying Gel+ to the face, eyebrows, eyelashes, beard, eyes, mouth, nose, broken skin, or other sensitive areas, and do not apply more than the prescribed amount. For a full overview of possible side effects, see the Gel+ medication overview. Contact your medical provider if you have any concerns about potential side effects.
