Frequently asked questions

What is Chew+?

Chew+ is a 5-in-1 compound prescription chewable tablet combining dutasteride, minoxidil, biotin, vitamin C, and zinc. It's the most effective oral Keeps formula — designed for progressive hair loss with a stronger DHT blocker and full nutrient support in one daily citrus-flavored chew.

How is Chew+ different from Chew?

Chew is a 3-in-1 formula with finasteride, minoxidil, and vitamin D3 — designed for early to moderate hair loss. Chew+ is a 5-in-1 formula with dutasteride (a stronger DHT blocker), minoxidil, biotin, vitamin C, and zinc — designed for progressive hair loss. Dutasteride blocks more forms of DHT than finasteride for more comprehensive coverage.

What's the difference between dutasteride and finasteride?

Both are DHT blockers used to treat male pattern hair loss. Finasteride blocks one type of the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT. Dutasteride blocks multiple types, making it a more comprehensive DHT blocker. Dutasteride is typically recommended for progressive hair loss where finasteride alone may not be sufficient.

Why a chew instead of a pill?

The chewable format allows Keeps to combine five active ingredients into a single daily dose — something that isn't possible with standard tablets. The citrus flavor makes it easy and pleasant to take consistently. No water needed, no multiple pills to manage.

Is Chew+ safe? What are the side effects?

Chew+ combines two prescription-strength medications — dutasteride and low-dose oral minoxidil — that are generally well-tolerated and considered safe for most men. Like all prescriptions, Chew+ may cause side effects in a small percentage of patients, including sexual side effects. Chew+ should never be taken by women. For a full overview of possible side effects, see the Chew+ medication overview. Contact your medical provider if you have any concerns about potential side effects.

What sort of results timeline should I expect?

Timelines vary by individual. Typically, shedding slows within months 1–3 as treatment activates at the follicle. Visible regrowth often begins around months 4–6. Peak results typically appear around 9–12 months, with density continuing to improve up to 18 months. Daily, consistent use is essential.
