Frequently asked questions‡

What is Minoxidil+?

Minoxidil+ is a signature Keeps compounded formula that pairs clinically proven ingredients that work together to improve hair health and boost regrowth. Topical minoxidil has long been known as a gold standard in treating hair loss and boosting regrowth by up to 35%. With added tretinoin, Minoxidil+ can be applied just once a day (vs. the standard twice-daily topical minoxidil application), which may make it easier to stay on track with treatment. Additionally, clinically proven topical caffeine and melatonin support thicker hair and improved scalp hair growth. The powerful benefits of these ingredients are combined in Keeps Minoxidil+ Hair Regrowth Spray.

How is Minoxidil+ different from other forms of minoxidil?

Minoxidil is a gold-standard treatment recommended by dermatologists to treat male pattern hair loss for decades. Other forms of minoxidil treatment include topical minoxidil spray and foam, oral minoxidil tablets, and other combination (compounded) treatments like Keeps Topical Finasteride + Minoxidil Gel.

Unlike some other forms of minoxidil, Minoxidil+ Spray is a topical formula that can be applied just once a day. The no-drip and quick-drying spray format allows for easy, mess-free application. Minoxidil+ also contains the clinically-proven hair loss benefits of caffeine and melatonin, plus tretinoin that enhances minoxidil’s effectiveness. This combination treatment allows for multiple hair loss treatments to work at once.

Can Minoxidil+ be used to prevent hair loss?

Like with all of the hair loss treatments offered and recommended by Keeps, preventing hair loss is often the first and most important step in keeping as much hair as possible, now and in the future. It helps to keep the hair you have with the clinically-proven effectiveness of ingredients like minoxidil, caffeine, and melatonin found in Minoxidil+.

What sort of results timeline should I expect with Minoxidil+?

As with all hair loss treatments, timelines to results can vary depending on the person. That said, as Minoxidil+ contains topical minoxidil, the results timeline is typically the same—with benefits showing up after 4-6 months after initial treatment. Daily, consistent, and continued application is recommended to retain results.

How does caffeine help hair loss?

Topical caffeine has been shown in clinical studies to support hair health, including improved hair thickness and decreased scalp dryness. Caffeine also supports healthy hair growth and makes thinning hair look and feel thicker.

How does melatonin help hair loss?

Topical melatonin has been shown in clinical studies to facilitate scalp hair growth, particularly in men experiencing male-pattern hair loss.

How does minoxidil help hair loss?

Minoxidil has been shown in clinical studies to boost hair regrowth by up to 35%, and is believed to work by increasing blood flow to hair follicles on the crown and top of the head.

What should I be aware of when using this hair and scalp treatment?

This compounded medication is typically well tolerated. Side effects usually occur at the start of therapy and decrease over time.

Common Side Effects:
• Minoxidil can initially cause a temporary increase in hair loss as it is working but this resolves in several weeks in most cases.
• Redness, peeling, dryness, or irritation of the skin where applied.
• Increased sensitivity to sunlight where applied. Sunscreen or other UV protection is recommended while on this medication.
• Tretinoin may cause changes in skin pigmentation at the sites of application.

Rare Side Effects:
• Some individuals may experience changes in hair color or texture.

This list may not describe all possible side effects. Call your provider for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.
