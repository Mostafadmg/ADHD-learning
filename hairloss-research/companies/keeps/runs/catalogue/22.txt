Frequently asked questions

What is saw palmetto?

Saw palmetto is a small palm tree native to the southeastern U.S. The fruit of the saw palmetto tree provides an extract that has been shown in clinical studies to help stabilize hair loss and promote healthy hair growth.

Can supplements help with hair loss?

Clinical studies show that the dosage of saw palmetto included in the Daily Hair Defense Supplement can help stabilize hair loss and promote healthy hair growth. A dietary supplement can be an easy and convenient way to start hair loss treatment (as it is available over-the-counter and does not require a prescription or consultation) or can be used in combination with your existing hair loss treatment plan. Please discuss your medical history including any medications you are currently taking with your healthcare provider. You can refer to product warnings and precautions here.

Was Daily Hair Defense Supplement formulated by doctors?

The vitamins and minerals included in Daily Hair Defense Supplement were specially selected by expert doctors at Keeps for men experiencing hair loss. The specific dosage of saw palmetto in Daily Hair Defense has been shown in clinical studies to help stabilize hair loss and promote healthy hair growth.

What precautions should I take when using Daily Hair Defense Supplement?

Please discuss your medical history including any medications you are currently taking with your healthcare provider. You can refer to product warnings and precautions here.

Consult your physician prior to using this product if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or have a medical condition. Infrequent, mild side effects, including headache, nausea, and dizziness, may occur with the use of saw palmetto. Saw palmetto might slow blood clotting and might cause extra bleeding during and after surgery. Stop using saw palmetto at least 2 weeks before a scheduled surgery due to concerns over possible increased bleeding risk. Taking saw palmetto along with medications that also slow blood clotting might increase the risk of bleeding and bruising. If you are currently taking blood thinners, anticoagulants (e.g. warfarin), antiplatelet medications (e.g. clopidogrel) or regularly taking aspirin or NSAIDs (e.g. ibuprofen, naproxen), please first discuss with your medical provider before using saw palmetto.

Taking high doses of biotin (>10 mg/day) may affect certain lab tests.  The dose of biotin contained in the Keeps Daily Hair Defense Supplement is .01 mg, and the recommended daily dietary intake of biotin is .03 mg/day. Please inform your healthcare providers if you are taking additional sources of biotin.
