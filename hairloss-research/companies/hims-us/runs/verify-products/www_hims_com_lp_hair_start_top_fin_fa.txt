Prevent hair loss

Finasteride works by blocking an enzyme that converts testosterone into DHT, a hormone that can weaken hair follicles.

Promote regrowth

Minoxidil kickstarts new growth by waking up dormant follicles and increasing how long they stay in the active growth phase.

Support healthy hair

Ketoconazole improves scalp health. It targets bacteria to clear the way for biotin to support healthy hair.
