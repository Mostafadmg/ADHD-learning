How finasteride & minoxidil work

Prevent hair loss

Finasteride works by blocking an enzyme that converts testosterone into DHT, a hormone that can weaken hair follicles.

Promote regrowth

Finasteride is used to help regrow hair in 3-6 months.*

Better together

Finasteride gives even better results when paired with minoxidil
