What to expect & when 
THE FIRST 2-3 MONTHS 
Due to the timing of the hair growth cycle, you may not see any changes in the first 2-3 months, but it is important to continue your treatment plan. Some may experience shedding in the first month of the treatment, but this is only temporary and completely normal. 
FROM 3-6 MONTHS 
You may notice that hair shedding has slowed or stopped and you may have spotted signs of regrowth. 
PLEASE NOTE: Results may vary between individuals, based on factors such as age, genetics and level of hair loss. 
AFTER 6 MONTHS 
The rate of hair shedding may have considerably slowed or stopped, and some men may experience signs of regrowth. 
PLEASE NOTE: Results may vary between individuals, based on factors such as age, genetics and level of hair loss.
