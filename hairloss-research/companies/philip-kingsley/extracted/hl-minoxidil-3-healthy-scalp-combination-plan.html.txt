What to expect & when 
THE FIRST 2-3 MONTHS 
Due to the timing of the hair growth cycle, you may not see any changes in the first 2-3 months, but it is important to continue your treatment plan. Some men may experience shedding in the first month of the treatment, but this is only temporary and completely normal. 
FROM 3-6 MONTHS

Frequently Asked Questions 
How does it work? 
Finasteride helps to prevent, maintain and possibly reverse the effects of Male Pattern Hair Loss by blocking the conversion of testosterone into dihydrotestosterone (more commonly known as DHT) which is the primary hormone that causes hair loss in men. 
Minoxidil works by shortening the telogen (shedding) phase and prolonging the anagen (growth) phase of the hair growth cycle. This ultimately helps to keep hair in place for longer and may promote new growth from dormant (kenogen) follicles.

How effective is it? 
Finasteride should maintain hair levels, slow or stop hair loss and in some cases may regrow some hair, regardless of your specific hair loss pattern. This treatment works best on mild to moderate hair thinning, which is why it’s important to start treating hair loss as soon as you notice the first signs. 
Studies have shown that over two thirds of patients with male pattern baldness saw an improvement in hair growth when taking Minoxidil. 
How do I use the plan? 
Take one tablet per day. It’s important to remember to be patient and keep up your course of treatment — your hair loss won’t stop immediately, and results are normally seen around three to six months after beginning a Finasteride treatment plan.
