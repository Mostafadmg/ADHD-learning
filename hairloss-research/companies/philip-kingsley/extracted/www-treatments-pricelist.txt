CONSULTATIONS 
Initial Consultation & Clinical Treatment 
If you are worried about hair loss, scalp issues, the condition of your hair, or if you have hair texture concerns or are experiencing hair breakage, we are here to help. During your 60-minute consultation, your trichologist will examine your hair and scalp, talk through your health history with you (think medication, diet and lifestyle habits, for example) and recommend a tailored treatment plan going forward. 
Sometimes blood tests may be recommended. These can usually be undertaken on the same day via our associated blood test lab (additional fees apply) or through your GP. A trichotherapist will then perform a bespoke treatment relevant to your diagnosis, during which you’ll be shown how to use your products, as well as given guidance as to how to style your hair healthily. 
Please note: More complex treatments may last more than 60 minutes, but your trichologist will advise on this. 
From £345

Virtual Initial Consultation 
If you are concerned about hair loss and/or your scalp health, but cannot visit our clinics in person, we are still able to help. During your consultation, your trichologist will discuss your concerns, then recommend a bespoke treatment plan and hair care regime. Blood tests may be recommended, which can often be arranged via your GP. 
The trichologist may not be able to provide a full diagnosis during your virtual consultation, but they will be able to share guidance/recommendations in terms of hair and scalp concerns. You may be asked to visit the clinic for a full assessment if needed — this will be discussed during your consultation. 
Please note: 
Based on the information you provide, the trichologist may not be able to give a definitive diagnosis, but they will be able to provide guidance relating to your hair/scalp concerns. 
The trichologist may suggest attending the clinic in-person for your follow-up appointment, in order to assess your hair or scalp and confirm diagnosis, as well as make any alterations to your treatment or product regimen.
