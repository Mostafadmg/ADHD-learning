Topical Scalp Drops 
Minoxidil 5% Scalp Drops are a topical solution to treat male and female pattern hair loss. When applied daily to the scalp, it helps promote hair growth by creating an optimal environment for dormant hair follicles. 
Supports healthy hair growth: Prolongs the anagen (growth) phase and reduces shedding 
Targets receding hairlines and thinning hair: Works effectively to restore hair density and volume 
Non-invasive treatment: Ideal for individuals seeking a topical solution rather than oral medication 
Daily use for best results: Consistency is essential for maximum effectiveness, with visible results often in 2-4 months 
Best for: 
Pattern hair loss requiring a targeted, topical treatment 
Receding hairlines and thinning hair that non-invasive treatment 
Daily hair loss treatment to reduce shedding 
Treatment seekers who prefer a non-oral solution for hair loss management 
Colour-treated hair that needs a safe, non-damaging solution for hair loss

What to expect & when 
THE FIRST 2-3 MONTHS 
Due to the timing of the hair growth cycle, you may not see any changes in the first 2-3 months, but it is important to continue your treatment plan. Some men may experience shedding in the first month of the treatment, but this is only temporary and completely normal. 
FROM 3-6 MONTHS 
You may notice that hair loss has slowed or stopped and you may have spotted signs of regrowth. 
PLEASE NOTE: Results may vary between individuals, based on factors such as age, genetics and level of hair loss. 
AFTER 6 MONTHS 
The rate of hair loss may have considerably slowed or stopped, and some men may experience signs of regrowth. 
PLEASE NOTE: Results may vary between individuals, based on factors such as age, genetics and level of hair loss.

Frequently Asked Questions 
Minoxidil 5% Scalp Drops work for hair loss treatment? 
Minoxidil works by shortening the telogen (shedding) phase and prolonging the anagen (growth) phase of the hair growth cycle. This ultimately helps to keep hair in place for longer and may promote new growth from dormant (kenogen) follicles. 
Can Minoxidil be used for female hair loss treatment? 
Yes, Minoxidil 5% is effective for both male and female pattern hair loss . It is commonly used to treat receding hairlines, thinning hair, and overall hair density in both men and women. 
How effective is it? 
Studies have shown that over two thirds of patients with male pattern baldness saw an improvement in hair growth. Visible results may take 2-4 months of consistent daily use. Some users may notice slower or faster results depending on individual factors. It's important to follow the treatment as directed for best results. 
Can I use Minoxidil alongside other hair care products? 
Yes, Minoxidil can be used in combination with other hair care products. However, we recommend using a gentle, nourishing shampoo to avoid scalp irritation and to maintain optimal conditions for hair growth. 
How do I apply the drops? 
Consistency is key with this treatment. 2.5ml should be applied to the affected areas of the scalp every day. We suggest applying this treatment at night before bed and shampooing the hair and scalp the following morning. Daily washing is imperative to prevent Minoxidil from building up on the scalp and reducing the effectiveness of the ingredients. We recommend shampooing with our Healthy Scalp Shampoo.
