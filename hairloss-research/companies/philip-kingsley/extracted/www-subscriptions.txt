What is Subscribe & Save? 
Subscribe & Save is the easiest and best value way to keep stocked up on your favourite Philip Kinglsey hair and scalp care products, with the convenience of automated recurring delivery straight to your door. 
When you subscribe you'll automatically save 20% off your first order and also save on all recurring orders after that. What's more is you'll also get free UK delivery on every recurring order. 
Subscribe & Save is quick and easy to set up and you'll always be in control of every order. Choose your subscription to be delivered either every 1, 2 or 3 months, and so you don't forget it's on it's way we'll send you a reminder 2 days before each order is due to be shipped. 
If you find you’re running out of your favourite products too quickly you can increase your delivery frequency or request your order is sent immediately. You can also pause or cancel your subscription at any time by logging into your account and visiting the 'Subscriptions' area. 
How do I start a subscription? 
To start your subscription, follow these 3 simple steps; 
1. Find your favourite products on our website and select the ‘Subscribe & Save’ option on the product page. 
2. Next, choose your delivery frequency from the dropdown and add the subscription to your bag. Your discount will be applied automatically at checkout. If you’re not ready to checkout then you can carry on shopping for other products, your subscription will be waiting for you in your bag. 
3. Once you’re ready to checkout, login or create an account and then click on the bag icon at the top right corner of the website. 
Once you have checked out you will receive two email confirmations. The first email will confirm our warehouse have received your order. The second email will confirm that your recurring subscription plan has been started. This will include a link to access your subscription settings for future deliveries. 
If you have any questions about starting a subscription, please contact our customer care team here . 
How do I get 20% off my first subscription order? 
Our Subscribe & Save 20% offer is available to new subscription customers. Simply login or create an account and the 20% discount will apply automatically at checkout on your first subscription order, you don't need to enter a discount code. 
If, after you've placed you first subscription order, you decide you want to start additional subscriptions, follow the same steps and your 15% discount will be automatically applied at checkout. 
If you have any questions about placing a subscription order, please contact our Customer Care Team here . 
Do I need an account to start a subscription? 
Yes, to be able to have full control over your subscriptions and future deliveries, you will need to either login or create a Philip Kingsley account before you checkout and pay for your subscription order. You can do this either at checkout or here . 
Can I change the date of my next subscription delivery? 
If you find your delivery frequency isn’t quite working for you, you can update your delivery date in the 'Subscriptions' area of your account here. 
If you have any questions about updating your subscription delivery date, please contact our customer care team . 
How do I pause or cancel my subscription? 
You can pause or cancel your subscription any time by logging into your account here. 
If you need help or have questions about your subscription, please contact our customer care team . 
How do I swap to another product? 
You can change the product in your subscription at any time through your account. Simply follow the steps below: 
Log in to your account on the Philip Kingsley website. 
Click Account and navigate to Subscriptions in the account menu. 
Find the subscription you would like to update. 
Click Swap Product next to the item. 
Select the product you would like to switch to and confirm the change. 
Your subscription will then continue with the newly selected product for the next scheduled order.
