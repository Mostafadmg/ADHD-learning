WHAT IS HAIR SHEDDING? 
Daily hair shedding is normal. On average, it is normal to lose up to 80-100 hairs per day, provided they are growing back. Yet in the case of telogen effluvium (excessive daily hair shedding), this can rise to as many as 300 being shed within a 24-hour period. 
WHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE? 
With telogen effluvium - excessive daily hair shedding - you will see an abnormal amount of hair fall on a daily basis. You may notice this when you wash your hair, or brush and style it. You may even notice it on your pillow. If left untreated, telogen effluvium can result in an overall loss of volume and the general feeling of having less hair than before. You may notice your ponytail is thinner, and/or that it is harder to create bounce or fullness in your style. 
WHAT IS FEMALE PATTERN HAIR LOSS? 
Female pattern hair loss is a progressive and slow type of hair loss. It means individual hairs are growing back thinner and shorter, with each hair growth cycle. Female pattern hair loss can occur any time after puberty. For many thinning most often begins or worsens around menopause, but it can also occur in younger women. 
WHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE? 
You won’t always notice extra hair fall, although shedding can co-exist alongside it. It is usually only noticeable once 15%-20% of hairs have already been affected and is most recognisable in women by a widening of the parting. 
Learn more about the causes and treatments for female hair loss, hair thinning and hair shedding. 
Hair loss in women is far more common than most people realise—and often goes undiagnosed or misunderstood. From pregnancy and menopause to hormonal imbalances, contraceptive use, stress, and poor nutrition, many factors can trigger female hair loss. The good news? With the right knowledge and care, it can often be treated and improved.
