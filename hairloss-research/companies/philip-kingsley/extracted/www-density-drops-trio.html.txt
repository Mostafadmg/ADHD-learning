Frequently Asked Questions 
We answer some common queries about Density Drops Trio. 
If you have more questions you can submit a question below or visit our Help Centre to speak with our Customer Care Team. 
How is this different to the Tricho 7 Scalp Drops I used to buy? 
Our Density Preserving Scalp Drops have replaced our Tricho 7 Scalp Drops in terms of what they treat and how they help you in your hair journey. But, there are a few changes... We've added new innovative anti-hair loss active ingredients and a cooling scalp stimulant, the texture in more of a serum texture as opposed to Tricho's water-thin consistency, and we've also removed alchohol from the formula so it's now purely water based. 
When should I apply the Preserving Scalp Drops? 
Use the Preserving Scalp Drops daily, ideally on a clean scalp, morning, or night, depending on your regime and personal preference. 
Will using the Preserving Scalp Drops more than recommended speed up hair growth? 
We recommend using the Preserving Scalp Drops once daily for optimal benefit. Using more of the product than directed or using the product more frequently will be unlikely to speed up results. 
How long will the Preserving Scalp Drops last? 
When used daily, an 85ml filled bottle should last a full month. This Trio should last 3 full months. 
Do the Drops contain Minoxidil? 
No, none of the products within our Density range contain Minoxidil. Minoxidil is a medicine, whereas the Density range is a collection of Cosmetic products.
