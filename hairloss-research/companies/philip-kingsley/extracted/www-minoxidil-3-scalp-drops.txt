Topical Scalp Drops 
Minoxidil 3% Scalp Drops is a clinically proven topical solution designed to treat male and female pattern hair loss. This effective treatment works by stimulating dormant hair follicles, creating an ideal environment for existing follicles to grow and thrive. Consistency is key with this treatment. Apply directly to the affected areas of the scalp daily to achieve the best results. 
Best for 
Male and female pattern hair loss 
Those seeking a topical solution for hair thinning and receding hairlines 
Daily use treatment that fits seamlessly into your routine 
Stimulating growth in dormant folicles for thicker, fuller hair 
More about Minoxidil 
How to apply 
Start with clean, towel-dried hair: Wash your hair and towel-dry it, leaving it damp. 
Measure 2.5ml: Use the dropper to measure 2.5ml of Minoxidil. 
Apply to the scalp: Part your hair and apply the solution directly to the areas with thinning hair or receding hairlines. Massage gently into the scalp with your fingertips. 
Do not rinse: Let the solution dry naturally. Do not rinse it out. 
Wash in the morning: Wash your hair with shampoo the next morning to remove any remaining product. 
Use daily: For the best results, apply Minoxidil 3% every day.

What to expect & when 
THE FIRST 2-3 MONTHS 
Due to the timing of the hair growth cycle, you may not see any changes in the first 2-3 months, but it is important to continue your treatment plan. Some men may experience shedding in the first month of the treatment, but this is only temporary and completely normal. 
FROM 3-6 MONTHS 
You may notice that hair loss has slowed or stopped and you may have spotted signs of regrowth. 
PLEASE NOTE: Results may vary between individuals, based on factors such as age, genetics and level of hair loss. 
AFTER 6 MONTHS 
The rate of hair loss may have considerably slowed or stopped, and some men may experience signs of regrowth. 
PLEASE NOTE: Results may vary between individuals, based on factors such as age, genetics and level of hair loss.

Frequently Asked Questions 
How do Minoxidil 3% Scalp Drops work? 
Minoxidil works by shortening the telogen (shedding) phase and prolonging the anagen (growth) phase of the hair growth cycle. This ultimately helps to keep hair in place for longer and may promote new growth from dormant (kenogen) follicles. 
How effective are Minoxidil 3% Scalp Drops? 
Minoxidil 3% Scalp Drops studies have shown that over two-thirds of men with male pattern baldness experienced improvements in hair growth. It’s also effective for female pattern hair loss, helping to restore hair thickness and volume. 
How should I use Minoxidil 3% Scalp Drops? 
Consistency is key with this treatment. 2.5ml should be applied to the affected areas of the scalp every day. We suggest applying this treatment at night before bed and shampooing the hair and scalp the following morning. Daily washing is imperative to prevent Minoxidil from building up on the scalp and reducing the effectiveness of the ingredients. We recommend shampooing with our Healthy Scalp Shampoo. 
Can I use this for receding hairlines or thinning hair? 
Our Minoxidil 3% Scalp Drops are highly effective for receding hairlines and thinning hair, making them an excellent option for both men and women looking to address these common hair concerns. Regular application can help promote thicker, fuller hair. 
Is Minoxidil suitable for both men and women? 
Yes, minoxidil 3% scalp drops are generally suitable for both male and female pattern hair loss. Whether you’re experiencing thinning hair or a receding hairline, this type of treatment can help stimulate hair regrowth and slow further hair loss in some users.
