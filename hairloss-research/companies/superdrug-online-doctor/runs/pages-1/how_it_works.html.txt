How It Works
Superdrug Online Doctor

Using Superdrug Online Doctor is like seeing your GP without having to actually see them.

Our doctors can advise and treat you, like your GP would, but using a simple and secure online medical questionnaire.

Treatments and test kits are dispensed by a Superdrug pharmacy and sent to your preferred UK address.

The healthcare you need can be in your hands in under 24 hours. And our doctors are always just a click away if you need any help or advice.

How it Works

1. Order Online

2. Doctor Approves

3. Dispense and Deliver

1. Order online
Choose a treatment or service.
Fill in a simple and secure medical questionnaire.
Register or log in, choose your delivery option, and pay.
2. Doctor approves
One of our doctors will review your questionnaire and decide if the treatment is right for you.
If the doctor needs more information, they’ll send you a message through your account or give you a call.
If the doctor’s not able to treat you they’ll explain why, give you advice on what to do next, and issue a full refund.
3. Dispense and deliver
All treatments are dispensed by a GPhC-registered Superdrug pharmacist.
Your order will come in a discreet package with nothing on it to show what’s inside.
4. Constant care
If you have any questions about your condition or the treatment or test kit that we’ve sent, you can contact one of our doctors through your account at any time for free.
Our UK-based Patient Care are on hand to help with any other queries you might have.
Your account

When you register, we’ll set up a free online patient account for you. Through this account, you can contact our doctors and our Patient Care, and it’s where you’ll find all of your order and treatment information.

You can access it on your computer, tablet or phone, in your own time from wherever you choose.

Secure and confidential

We understand that your medical information is sensitive. We host our systems with AWS, the industry leader for security. Our doctors practise from our London office.

You can find out more about how we protect your personal information in our Privacy Notice.

About our practice

Responsible Pharmacist

You can find out who the Responsible Pharmacist was when your order was processed in two ways:

If you collected your order from a Superdrug store with a pharmacy, you can check the Superdrug Store Finder to find the relevant contact details.
If your order was delivered, or you collected from a Superdrug store without a pharmacy or post office, please contact our Patient Care directly.
