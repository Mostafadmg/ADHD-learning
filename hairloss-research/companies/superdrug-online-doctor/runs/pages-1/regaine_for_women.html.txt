About Regaine for Women
What is Regaine for women?

Regaine for Women is a treatment used for hereditary hair loss or hair thinning in women between the ages of 18 and 65.

It works best in women with generally thinning hair but is unlikely to work for younger women or those who have been losing hair for a short length of time.

How do I use Regaine for women?

Your hair should be dry before using Regaine for Women.

Apply a single dose of 1ml twice a day to the affected areas of the scalp, using one of the applicators provided. Make sure to leave a gap of 12 hours in-between treatments and do not exceed the recommended dosage of 2ml per day.

It can take up to 16 weeks before any hair regrowth is noticed. Continued hair growth will require continuous treatment with Regaine for Women. If you do not notice an improvement within 12 months of using Regaine for Women, stop using this product.

Can I use Regaine?

Regaine for Women is for use by women with hereditary hair loss or who have general thinning of the hair. You can tell if you have hereditary hair loss if you:

have the recognizable pattern of hair loss (described in the product info leaflet)
have a family history of hair loss
have hair loss that starts gradually and gets progressively worse
have no other symptoms with your hair loss

If you have been experiencing hair loss over a very short period of time, or have been balding for many years, it is unlikely that you will benefit from using this product.

Are there side effects?

Possible side effects of Regaine for Women may include:

chest pain
increased heart rate
dizziness or fainting
unexplained weight gain
swelling of the feet or hands
persistent redness of the scalp

Within the first few weeks of treatment with Regaine for Women, it is common to experience increased hair shedding. However, if this continues for more than 2 weeks, you should stop using Regaine for Women.

Do not use Regaine for Women if you are allergic to any of the listed ingredients, and make sure to read the leaflet that comes with your medication before you use it.
