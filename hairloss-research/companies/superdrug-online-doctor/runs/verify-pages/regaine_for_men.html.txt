About Regaine For Men
What is Regaine For Men?

Regaine for Men is a treatment for male pattern baldness, otherwise known as androgenic alopecia. Male pattern hair loss is the most common form of hair loss in men.

It happens in men whose hair roots are more sensitive to the hormone androgen, which causes the hair roots to shrink and makes it harder for them to grow normal hair.

Regaine for Men contains minoxidil and it can be used to prevent further hair loss and to initiate hair regrowth. Clinical studies have found that around 3 in 5 men reported hair regrowth after 48 weeks of using Regaine.

How do I use Regaine For Men

Your hair should be dry before you use Regaine for Men.

Apply a single dose of 1ml, using one of the applicators provided, to the affected area twice a day. Leave a gap of 12 hours in-between treatments and do not exceed the recommended dose of 2ml per day.

It can take up to 8 weeks before any hair regrowth is noticed. Continued hair growth requires continuous treatment with Regaine for Men. If you notice no improvement within 16 weeks, stop using this product.

Regaine foam dries quickly and it will not interfere with your styling products.

When using Regaine solution, wait until your scalp has dried before you apply any other products.

Should I try Regaine?

Regaine for Men should be used by men who are experiencing male pattern hair loss (androgenic alopecia) and are hoping to stimulate hair regrowth and/or prevent further hair loss.

Can Regaine cause side effects?

Possible side effects can include:

headaches
skin rash or irritation on application
low blood pressure levels (hypotension)
changes in hair colour or texture

It is possible that Regaine for Men will increase hair shedding at the beginning of the treatment course. This is normal and can occur in the first few weeks. However, if this continues for longer than 2 weeks, stop using this product.

Do not use Regaine for Men if you are allergic to minoxidil or any of the listed ingredients, and always read the leaflet that comes with your medication. Stop using this product and call 999 if you experience any of the following:

increased heart rate
chest pain
fainting or dizziness
lip/ tongue or face swelling
Itchy rash
difficulty breathing, chest tightness or shortness of breath

You must see your doctor quickly if you have any of the following:

swelling in the hands or feet
unexplained weight gain
long-lasting redness of the scalp
