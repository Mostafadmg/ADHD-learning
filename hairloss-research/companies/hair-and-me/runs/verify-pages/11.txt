How Hair + Me works
Why hair loss happens

Genetic predisposition causes follicle shrinkage. Vitamin and mineral deficiencies, lifestyle and hormonal changes affect the hair growth cycle.

How we prevent hair loss

Through drops and capsules, our scientifically proven ingredients stimulate blood flow and block dihydrotestosterone (DHT), the hair loss hormone.

How hair regrows

Our powerful formulations work to rebalance nutrient levels, nourish the scalp and restore follicle function – accelerating the hair regrowth cycle.

How we help you

Our Dermatology Clinicians personally review your consultation to build a treatment plan that's personalised to you. We'll check in regularly to make sure you're on track.
