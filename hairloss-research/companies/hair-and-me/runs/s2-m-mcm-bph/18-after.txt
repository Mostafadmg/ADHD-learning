Understanding Dutasteride and Finasteride

I acknowledge that:

These medications are not suitable if planning to start a family soon. I confirm that I am not planning to start a family in the next six months and agree to inform Hair + Me if my plans change.
These medications may be present in semen, so if my partner is pregnant or breastfeeding, I’ll use protection (like a condom) as a precaution.
Taking these medications may cause mood changes. It is uncommon, but this may include depression or suicidal thoughts. If this happens, I should stop my treatment, contact my GP immediately, and inform Hair + Me.
A temporary decrease in libido or changes in erectile function may occur. If this happens, I will pause my treatment and contact Hair + Me for guidance.
Mild breast tenderness or enlargement are possible side effects. If I notice any changes, I will stop treatment, contact my GP immediately, and inform Hair + Me.
In rare cases, some side effects may continue after stopping treatment. I will read the Patient Information Leaflet and contact Hair + Me if I have any concerns.
