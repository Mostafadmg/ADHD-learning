Do you live with pets?
Yes
No

Why are we asking?

Some of our treatments contain minoxidil, which is toxic to cats and dogs. The main risk is them licking it off fingers/hair after use.

Capsules are less likely to have a risk, as the product doesn’t sit on your hair in the same way. They’re still toxic if your pet ingests the capsule.

Keep all medication out of reach of your pets.
