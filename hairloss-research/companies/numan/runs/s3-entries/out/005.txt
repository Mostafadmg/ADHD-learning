What causes hair loss?

Hair loss becomes more common as you age, but hair loss in young men is common too. There are several types of hair loss. The most common is male pattern baldness, which is mainly caused by genetics and the hormone dihydrotestosterone (DHT).

How can I treat hair loss?

Some treatments prevent hair loss, especially when used early. Finasteride is a medication that targets the root cause of male pattern baldness. Minoxidil is an alternative treatment that protects the hair at the follicles. They can also be used together for the best results.

The science behind hair loss treatments

Discover how clinically proven hair loss treatments counteract two main causes of male pattern baldness: dihydrotestosterone (DHT) and shrinking follicles.

Why is hair lost?

DHT shrinks your hair follicles over time, causing hair to become thinner and shorter, eventually stopping growth altogether.

How finasteride helps

Finasteride reduces DHT, a hormone that causes hair follicles to shrink over time. This can help slow hair loss and, for some men, help hair grow back.4

How minoxidil helps

Minoxidil reactivates damaged follicles by increasing the supply of blood and nutrients. This helps your scalp replace thin, weak hairs with new, stronger ones.

FAQS
Your questions answered
What should I expect when I use a treatment for hair loss?

Hair regrowth is a naturally slow process, so there may not be much visible change for the first one to three months.

After about three to six months of using your hair loss treatment as prescribed, hair loss should start to slow down and newly regrown hairs should begin to emerge. Initial hairs may be soft and light at the start, but should eventually thicken.

Beyond six months, hair loss should slow significantly and thicker, fuller regrown hair should begin to fill in patches of thinning hair. Scalp coverage should noticeably improve and hairs should look stronger and healthier.

How does finasteride work?

Finasteride helps to counter male pattern baldness by reducing the action of DHT (dihydrotestosterone) on hair follicles. DHT is a naturally occurring molecule that interacts with hair follicles in a way that causes the growth phase of the hair cycle to gradually decrease. This shrinks the follicle resulting in thinner, shorter hairs until the growth phase is so short that the hairs don’t grow from the scalp at all.

Finasteride stops the production of DHT by inhibiting 5-alpha-reductase, the enzyme which converts testosterone into DHT, meaning that less DHT is present in the scalp (around 60% less). This helps to prevent further hair loss, and in many cases, promotes hair regrowth.

How does minoxidil work?

Minoxidil can reduce and reverse male hair loss by:

Widening the blood vessels and allowing them to supply nutrient-rich blood to the hair follicles - increasing hair density and promoting hair regrowth.

Prolonging the anagen phase of the hair growth cycle. The anagen phase (known as the “growth” stage of the hair cycle) is the stage in which new hair is generated.

Increasing hair follicle size and hair diameter, which can make hair appear thicker and fuller.

Been using minoxidil for months without results? Find out why minoxidil might not be working and what to do next.

What is the best hair loss treatment?

A clinician will recommend the safest, most effective treatment for you based on your needs, preferences, and lifestyle.

Finasteride is a daily tablet that slows hair loss and promotes regrowth. It works well as a receding hairline treatment, and is also a popular alternative for those who have tried minoxidil but didn’t get the results they wanted.

Finasteride and minoxidil can also be used together. The two treatments tackle hair loss in different ways, and combining them has been shown to improve hair loss in 94% of men.1

Regaine is another hair loss treatment that can be used to target the areas of the scalp most affected by male pattern hair loss. Its active ingredient minoxidil has been clinically proven to stimulate hair regrowth, and Regaine comes with three different applicators to help you apply precisely the right amount for hair growth.

Are hair loss treatments suitable for everyone?

Hair loss treatments are suitable for men aged 18-65 who are experiencing mild to moderate male pattern hair loss.

Finasteride and Regaine For Men Extra Strength are not suitable for women. A full list of warnings and precautions is available in the patient information leaflet included with your treatment, which you should always read before starting treatment for the first time.

What are the potential side effects of hair loss treatments?

Like most medications, hair loss treatments can cause side effects, although not everyone gets them. Before using any medication for hair loss it’s important to be aware of the potential side effects, as this knowledge will help you to use your hair loss treatment safely and effectively.

Minoxidil

The common side effects include:

Itching and/or skin irritation of the scalp at the site of application.

If you experience any of the side effects mentioned above stop using the hair loss treatment and seek medical guidance.

Finasteride

Sometimes, men taking finasteride 1mg experience changes in their sexual function. In trials, only around 1.5% of men experienced symptoms such as erectile dysfunction, decreased libido, and ejaculatory dysfunction. Stopping treatment usually leads to the resolution of these symptoms in over 99% of men, but in a small number of cases it may be irreversible.

Other rare side effects of finasteride include testicular pain, depression, thoughts or acts of suicide and breast tissue growth/tenderness.

A complete list of side effects is available in the patient information leaflet included with every treatment we provide.

How long do hair loss treatments take to work?

The time it takes to see a reduction in hair loss and the regrowth of new hair depends on the type of hair loss treatment you choose. As hair growth is a naturally slow process, it usually takes a few months to notice any changes. This is normal, and the best results are usually observed after six months of treatment.

Finasteride: Takes about three to six months to notice reduced hair loss and/or the regrowth of hair.

Regaine: Takes about two to four months to notice reduced hair loss and/or the regrowth of hair.

You should bear in mind that these timeframes are guidelines, because the time it takes for a male pattern baldness treatment to work is likely to differ from person to person. Some people may experience noticeable changes to their hair in a shorter timeframe than the ones described above. Likewise, it may take a little longer for others. Therefore, it’s best to stay consistent with your chosen hair loss treatment, as hair regrowth tends to improve the longer a treatment is used.

What happens if I stop using my hair loss treatment?

If you decide to stop using your hair loss treatment, it’s likely that you will lose the hair you regrew during treatment within three to four months. Therefore, continuous use is recommended for the best results.

To help you stay on track, we’ll send you a refill of your hair loss treatment every month so you never have to worry about running out. By keeping topped up, you can retain the hair you’ve regrown and continue to encourage new growth.

What happens if I forget to use my hair loss treatment one day?

Don’t worry if you forget to use your hair loss treatment one day. Although male pattern baldness treatments work best when they’re used consistently, it takes about three to four months for regrown hair to disappear once treatment is stopped. Therefore, forgetting to use the treatment for one day shouldn’t have an impact on your hair coverage.

Important: If you forget to take your hair loss treatment, do not double the dose to make up for the one/s you missed. Instead, use it as normal the following day.
