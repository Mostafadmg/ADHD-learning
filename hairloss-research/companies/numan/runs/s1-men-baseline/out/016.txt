How to use Medicated Spray

When using a hair regrowth treatment such as Medicated Spray, it’s important to use it correctly for the best results. Using it properly will also help you to use it safely, which is key when applying any hair loss treatment to your hair.

For the best regrowth results, it’s recommended that Medicated Spray is used twice a day (once in the morning and once at night).

To use Medicated Spray
In the morning:
Make sure the scalp is completely dry before applying the spray.
Aim the spray nozzle over the centre of the bald area of the scalp.
Spray the solution once and spread the liquid with your fingertips to cover the bald area.
Repeat a further five times to apply a dose of 1ml of the solution.
Wash your hands thoroughly after use.
At night:

Perform steps 1-5 as described above.

Do not use more than 2ml of Medicated Spray daily. Exceeding the recommended dose will not regrow your hair any faster and you may increase the risk of side effects.

Side effects to be aware of
Mental health: Finasteride can cause depression and suicidal thoughts. If you experience low mood or thoughts of self-harm, stop taking finasteride and contact your doctor immediately. If you are at risk of serious harm, call 999.
Sexual dysfunction: Decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, and ejaculation problems can occur. These may happen without any change in mood.
Persistence: Both mental health and sexual side effects may continue even after you stop taking finasteride. Report any side effects to your clinician promptly.
Fertility: Do not take finasteride if you are trying to conceive.

Full safety information and instructions are provided with your Medicated Spray, which you should read thoroughly before use.

How to take finasteride

Take one tablet each day. It usually takes about 3-6 months to notice an increase in hair density or reduction in hair loss when using finasteride. The best results are normally observed after 6 months of treatment.

Finasteride will not work faster or better if you take it more than once a day.

If you forget to take your tablet on a given day, do not take a double dose the next day.

Full safety information and instructions are provided with your Finasteride, which you should read thoroughly before use.

How to take the Hair Growth Support Supplement

Take one tablet once per day. Do not exceed the stated dose. This product contains vitamin A. Do not take if you are pregnant or expecting to become pregnant except on the advice of a doctor.

Full ingredients list, instructions and safety information are provided with your Hair Growth Support Supplement, which you should read thoroughly before use.
