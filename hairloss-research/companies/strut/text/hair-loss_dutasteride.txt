Frequently Asked Questions
What is Dutasteride?
Dutasteride is a prescription 5-alpha reductase inhibitor that blocks both Type I and Type II enzymes responsible for converting testosterone into DHT, the hormone most associated with male pattern hair loss.*
How does Dutasteride work for hair loss?
Dutasteride may reduce DHT levels in the scalp by approximately 90%, lowering the hormonal signal that drives follicle miniaturization, which may help slow the visible thinning that follows.*
How long does Dutasteride take to show results?
It may take 3 to 6 months to begin seeing results, and up to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
Can I take Dutasteride and Finasteride together?
Typically no. The two medications act on the same pathway and are not commonly combined. Your Strut provider will choose one or the other based on your goals and tolerance.
What happens if I stop taking Dutasteride?
DHT levels return to baseline over time after stopping, and the hair benefits achieved while on therapy will gradually be lost. Ongoing use is required to maintain results.
Is Dutasteride FDA-approved for hair loss?
Dutasteride is FDA-approved for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), not for hair loss. Use for hair loss is supervised by a licensed provider. Compounded dutasteride is not FDA-approved.
What is the difference between oral and topical Dutasteride?
Oral dutasteride is a daily capsule that lowers DHT systemically. Topical dutasteride is applied directly to the scalp and may reduce systemic exposure while still acting on the follicle*. Your healthcare provider will help you choose between them.
Will Dutasteride affect my testosterone levels?
Dutasteride lowers DHT, not testosterone. Side effects can include decreased libido. See the safety information on this page, and discuss any concerns with your healthcare provider.
What are the side effects of Dutasteride?
Decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, decreased semen volume, breast tenderness or enlargement, and mood changes. See the safety information on this page.

The online visits are typically complementary. You only pay for the medication if a Strut provider determines treatment is appropriate.
