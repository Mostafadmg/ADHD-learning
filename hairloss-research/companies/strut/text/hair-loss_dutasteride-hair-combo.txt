However, if you missed a dose and it has been more than a few hours skip your dose and continue on like usual the next day. Dutasteride will not work better if you take more than 1 daily, so take Dutasteride capsules exactly as prescribed and never take an extra dose. How do Dutasteride and Finasteride differ?
Dutasteride and finasteride are both 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors that reduce the conversion of testosterone to DHT. Dutasteride inhibits types 1, 2 and 3, while finasteride primarily inhibits types 2 and 3 (Yamana et al., Hormone Molecular Biology and Clinical Investigation, 2010). A healthcare provider can help determine which treatment may be appropriate based on your individual needs.
How does each Dutasteride Custom Hair Loss Formula work?
Compounded Topical Dutasteride Formula: Strut Men's Dutasteride Hair Formula is a compounded prescription topical that may include dutasteride along with other prescription and nonprescription ingredients selected by a licensed healthcare provider based on your individual needs. Dutasteride is a 5-alpha reductase inhibitor that may help lower DHT, an androgen associated with male pattern hair loss.* Minoxidil has been studied for its role in hair growth. Does topical Dutasteride have good scalp absorption?
Dutasteride is a larger molecule than Finasteride, so it may not absorb into the scalp as readily as topical Finasteride formulations. To help with this, our compounded topical Dutasteride formula is made with DMSO, which may help the formula penetrate the scalp, and may include Tretinoin, which has been studied for its potential to increase absorption of topical hair loss ingredients like Minoxidil. Some patients use the formula along with a microneedling device, which may help enhance penetration. Ask your provider whether microneedling is appropriate for you.
If I'm currently using another hair loss product, will a switch cause shedding?
Typically, there is no shedding period if you are switching from an oral Finasteride or Dutasteride to topical Finasteride or Dutasteride, or from a topical to an oral form. However, if you have been using a topical minoxidil product and you are switching to a medication that does not contain Minoxidil, you may see shedding. To avoid temporary shedding when switching, you can gradually taper off your minoxidil over 6 to 8 weeks, or simply ensure minoxidil is included in your new custom blend.
