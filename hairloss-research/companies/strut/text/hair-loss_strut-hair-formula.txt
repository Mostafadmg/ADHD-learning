Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Strut Hair Formula?
Strut Women's Hair Growth Formula is a provider-prescribed compounded topical that combines Spironolactone, Finasteride, Minoxidil, Tretinoin, and Biotin in a single nightly dropper. It is designed to be personalized to your scalp and your goals.*
What are the active ingredients in the Strut Hair Formula?
Spironolactone, Finasteride, Minoxidil, Tretinoin, and Biotin in a compounded topical base. Fluocinolone may also be included based on your provider's recommendation.
How does the Strut Hair Formula work?
Spironolactone and Finasteride may help block DHT at the follicle. Minoxidil may support blood flow and nutrient delivery to the scalp. Tretinoin and Biotin may support absorption and hair shaft strength.*
How do I apply the Strut Hair Formula?
Apply daily to dry, clean hair. Wash your hands after application. Allow the formula to dry thoroughly, then wash it off in the morning or after at least 4 hours.
Can the Strut Hair Formula be customized?
Yes. The formula is compounded to your prescription. A Strut provider reviews your intake and may adjust the active strengths and ingredients to fit your scalp, your goals, and your tolerance.
How long does it take to see results?
It may take 2 to 4 months to begin seeing results, and up to 6 to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
What should I tell my healthcare provider before starting the formula?
Share any history of scalp conditions, sensitivities to topical medications, current prescriptions, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, and any history of low blood pressure or fainting.
What are the potential side effects?
Itching, burning, redness, flaking, dryness, scaling, peeling, stinging, changes in hair color or texture, irritation at the application site, and increased sun sensitivity. See the safety information on this page.
Can I use the Hair Formula with other treatments?
It depends on the treatment. Share any topical or oral hair-loss treatments you already use with your Strut provider, who can review your routine.
Who should NOT use the Strut Hair Formula?
Do not use if you are pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or breastfeeding. Finasteride-containing medications carry risk of fetal harm and must be avoided in those scenarios.
How much does the online visit cost?
The online visits are typically complimentary. You only pay for the medication if a Strut provider determines treatment is appropriate.
