Frequently Asked Questions
What is Oral Finasteride?
Oral Finasteride is a once-daily 1mg tablet that blocks Type II 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that converts testosterone into DHT. It is FDA-approved for the treatment of male pattern hair loss.*
How does Finasteride work for hair loss?
Finasteride may reduce DHT levels by approximately 70%, lowering the hormonal signal that drives follicle miniaturization, which may help slow the visible thinning that follows.*
How does Oral Finasteride compare to Topical?
Oral Finasteride works systemically and produces consistent, body-wide DHT reduction. Compounded topical finasteride is used for male pattern hair loss.* Your Strut provider will help you decide which fits your goals.
How long does Finasteride take to work?
It may take 3 to 6 months to begin seeing results, and up to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
What is the difference between Finasteride and Dutasteride?
Finasteride blocks Type 2 5-alpha reductase only, while Dutasteride blocks both Type 1 and Type 2. Your healthcare provider will help decide which option is appropriate for you.
What happens if I stop taking Finasteride?
DHT levels return to baseline over time after stopping, and the hair benefits achieved while on therapy will gradually be lost. Ongoing use is required to maintain results.
Is Finasteride FDA-approved for hair loss?
Yes. Oral Finasteride 1mg is FDA-approved for the treatment of male pattern hair loss.
Can I use Finasteride with other hair loss treatments?
Share any topical or oral hair-loss treatments you already use with your Strut provider, who can review your routine.
What are the side effects of Finasteride?
Decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, decreased semen volume, breast tenderness or enlargement, and mood changes. See the safety information on this page.
How much does the online visit cost?
The online visits are typically complimentary. You only pay for the medication if a Strut provider determines treatment is appropriate.
Is shipping free?
Yes. Discreet & Free Shipping is included with every order, along with healthcare provider consultations and follow-ups.
What is the important safety information?
Finasteride is for adult men only. It must not be handled by women who are or may become pregnant due to risk of fetal harm. Common side effects include changes in libido, erectile function, semen volume, breast tissue, and mood. Discuss your full medical history with your Strut provider before starting.
