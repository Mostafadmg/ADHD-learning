Frequently Asked Questions
What is HairfectRx with Finasteride?
HairfectRx with Finasteride is a once-daily compounded liposomal capsule that combines Finasteride 1mg with oral Minoxidil 5mg and 6 essential hair vitamins (D-Biotin, Vitamin C, Methyltetrahydrofolate, Vitamin D3, Zinc Picolinate, Vitamin A). One capsule covers DHT blocking, follicle stimulation, and nutritional support.*
How does Finasteride work for hair loss?
Finasteride is a Type II 5-alpha reductase inhibitor that blocks the conversion of testosterone into DHT, the hormone most associated with male pattern hair loss. It may reduce DHT levels by approximately 70%.*
How does the liposomal delivery system work?
Liposomal delivery wraps the actives in lipid vesicles that may improve absorption and bioavailability*. This is intended to deliver more of each active ingredient to the bloodstream and follicle.
Do I need additional supplements with HairfectRx Finasteride?
Typically no. The capsule already includes Biotin, Vitamin C, Methyltetrahydrofolate, Vitamin D3, Zinc Picolinate, and Vitamin A. Talk to your Strut provider before stacking other hair supplements.
Can I take HairfectRx with other hair loss treatments?
It depends on the treatment. Share any topical or oral hair-loss treatments you already use with your Strut provider, who can review your routine.
How long does it take to see results?
It may take 3 to 6 months to begin seeing results, and up to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
What should I tell my healthcare provider before starting?
Share any history of liver disease, prostate conditions, low blood pressure, heart conditions, current prescriptions, and any plans for blood donation while on finasteride.
What are the potential side effects?
Decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, decreased semen volume, breast tenderness or enlargement, mood changes, and minoxidil-related side effects such as fluid retention, dizziness, or unwanted body hair growth. See the safety information on this page.
How much does the online visit cost?
The online visits are typically complimentary. You only pay for the medication if a Strut provider determines treatment is appropriate.
Is shipping free?
Yes. Discreet & Free Shipping is included with every order, along with healthcare provider consultations and follow-ups.
What is the important safety information?
HairfectRx is for adult men only. It must not be handled by women who are or may become pregnant due to risk of fetal harm. Side effects can include changes in libido, erectile function, mood, blood pressure, and unwanted body hair growth. Discuss your full medical history with your Strut provider before starting.
