Frequently Asked Questions
What is Topical Finasteride?
Topical Finasteride is a provider-prescribed compounded gel that combines Finasteride, Minoxidil, Tretinoin, Fluocinolone, and Biotin in a topical base. It targets hair loss from multiple angles in a single nightly application to the scalp.*
How do I apply Topical Finasteride?
Apply daily to dry, clean scalp where thinning is visible. Wash your hands after application. Allow the formula to dry thoroughly, then wash off in the morning or after at least 4 hours.
Is Topical Finasteride FDA-approved?
Oral Finasteride 1mg is FDA-approved for male pattern hair loss. Compounded Topical Finasteride is not FDA-approved and is prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy under healthcare provider supervision.
How long does Finasteride take to work?
It may take 3 to 6 months to begin seeing results, and up to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
What is the difference between Finasteride and Dutasteride?
Finasteride blocks Type 2 5-alpha reductase only, while Dutasteride blocks both Type 1 and Type 2. Your healthcare provider will help decide which option is appropriate for you.
What happens if I stop taking Finasteride?
DHT levels return to baseline over time after stopping, and the hair benefits achieved while on therapy will gradually be lost. Ongoing use is required to maintain results.
Can I use Topical Finasteride with other treatments?
Share any topical or oral hair-loss treatments you already use with your Strut provider, who can review your routine.
What are the side effects of Finasteride?
Itching, burning, redness, flaking, dryness, scaling, peeling, stinging, changes in hair color or texture, irritation at the application site, and increased sun sensitivity. See the safety information on this page.
How much does the online visit cost?
The online visits are typically complimentary. You only pay for the medication if a Strut provider determines treatment is appropriate.
Is shipping free?
Yes. Discreet & Free Shipping is included with every order, along with healthcare provider consultations and follow-ups.
Who is a good candidate for topical Finasteride?
Adult men experiencing male pattern hair loss with active follicles still present, particularly those who prefer a topical to an oral medication. Your Strut provider will review your intake and medical history to determine whether this formula is appropriate for you.
