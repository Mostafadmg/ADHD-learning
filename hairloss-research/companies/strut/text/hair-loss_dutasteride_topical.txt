Frequently Asked Questions
What is Topical Dutasteride?
Topical Dutasteride Formula is a provider-prescribed compounded gel that combines Dutasteride, Minoxidil, Tretinoin, Fluocinolone, and Biotin in a topical base. It targets hair loss from multiple angles in a single nightly application.*
How do I apply Topical Dutasteride?
Apply daily to dry, clean scalp where thinning is visible. Wash your hands after application. Allow the formula to dry thoroughly, then wash off in the morning or after at least 4 hours.
Can I use Topical Dutasteride with other hair loss treatments?
Share any topical or oral hair-loss treatments you already use with your Strut provider. They can review your routine with you.
How long does Dutasteride take to show results?
It may take 3 to 6 months to begin seeing results, and up to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
What happens if I stop taking Dutasteride?
DHT levels return to baseline over time after stopping, and the hair benefits achieved while on therapy will gradually be lost. Ongoing use is required to maintain results.
How does Topical compare to Oral Dutasteride?
Topical Dutasteride acts more locally at the scalp and may reduce systemic exposure. Oral Dutasteride works systemically and produces a higher level of overall DHT reduction. Your Strut provider will help you decide which fits your goals.
Will Dutasteride affect my testosterone levels?
Dutasteride lowers DHT, not testosterone. Side effects can include decreased libido. See the safety information on this page, and discuss any concerns with your healthcare provider.
What are the side effects of Dutasteride?
Itching, burning, redness, flaking, dryness, scaling, peeling, stinging, changes in hair color or texture, irritation at the application site, and increased sun sensitivity. See the safety information on this page.
How much does the online visit cost?
The online visits are typically complimentary. You only pay for the medication if a Strut provider determines treatment is appropriate.
Is shipping free?
Yes. Discreet & Free Shipping is included with every order, along with healthcare provider consultations and follow-ups.
What is the important safety information?
Topical Dutasteride is for adult men only. It must not be handled by women who are or may become pregnant due to risk of fetal harm. Common side effects are localized scalp reactions. Discuss your full medical history with your Strut provider before starting.
