What is Strut Hair Booster?
Strut Hair Booster is a prescription topical formula combining latanoprost, melatonin, caffeine, and biotin in a gentle topical base, designed to support fuller-looking hair and can be combined with treatments like finasteride, dutasteride, or minoxidil.

How to Use Strut Hair Booster
Topical hair loss medications are to be applied to the scalp only. Do not take by mouth.

Store at room temperature, away from moisture and heat.

Can I use Strut Hair Booster along with Finasteride or Dutasteride?
A clinician may prescribe Strut Hair Booster together with oral or topical Finasteride or Dutasteride for you. Finasteride / Dutasteride directly target DHT, whereas Latanoprost, Melatonin and Caffeine are used to address hair concerns not associated with DHT reduction. Whether combination treatment is appropriate depends on your current medications, formulation, medical history, and treatment goals.
How do I use Strut Hair Booster if I am already using a topical Finasteride or Dutasteride formula?
Strut Hair Booster can generally be incorporated into your daily routine alongside a topical Finasteride or Dutasteride treatment. Choose a convenient time once daily to apply Strut Hair Booster (many users apply it at the same time as their other topical formula for consistency). Apply a thin layer of Strut Hair Booster directly to the scalp immediately after applying your Finasteride or Dutasteride solution. You may layer them back-to-back or allow a few minutes between applications for the first product to absorb, depending on your personal preference.
When can I expect to see results from Strut Hair Booster?
Hair-loss treatment generally requires consistent use over time. Published studies of individual topical ingredients used in hair-loss treatment have evaluated outcomes over several months (some studies of the individual ingredients have shown change around 6 months), but response to this compounded formulation may vary. Your clinician can help assess your response over time. Compounded products are not FDA-approved, and FDA has not evaluated them for safety, effectiveness, or quality.
Can men and women use Strut Hair Booster?
Yes! Strut Hair Booster was created as a unisex formula that can be used in males or females, alone or in conjunction with other treatments. Some common hair loss treatment options may be predominantly used in one gender over the other since they focus on hormones. For instance, Dutasteride and Finasteride are normally used for male hair loss patients, while Spironolactone tends to be used in female hair loss patients only. But, Strut Hair Booster only combines active ingredients that do not have a hormonal focus and can be used in both genders. Strut Hair Booster may also be a good option for people who want to treat their hair loss, but do not want to use a DHT-targeting therapy. (Let your healthcare provider know if you are a woman who is pregnant or breastfeeding, as this formula has not been tested in these populations and should be avoided.)
How does Strut Hair Booster work?
Strut Hair Booster is a customized, compounded topical formulation that may combine key active ingredients such as Latanoprost, Melatonin, Caffeine, and Biotin. Latanoprost has been evaluated in clinical studies for its potential to support the hair growth phase and improve visible density. Caffeine has been studied for its ability to help protect hair follicles against environmental stressors and support scalp circulation. Melatonin & Biotin are ingredients included to support scalp health, hair strength, and overall hair health. These studies do not establish the safety or effectiveness of every compounded combination or strength. Your clinician will determine whether this formulation is appropriate for you.
Will I feel tired from the melatonin in this medication?
Topical melatonin is intended for application to the scalp, and systemic absorption may differ from oral melatonin. If you experience drowsiness or any unexpected effect, contact us.
Will I feel energized from the caffeine in this medication?
The caffeine in this formulation is applied topically to the scalp. Systemic exposure may be limited, but individual response can vary. If you notice restlessness, palpitations, or other unexpected effects, contact us.
How should I use Strut Hair Booster?
Use the medicine exactly as directed. Using more medicine or applying it more often than prescribed will not make it work any faster, and may increase side effects. Do not take this medication by mouth or use it in or around your eyes. Avoid getting this medication in your eyes or mouth, if this does occur, rinse with water. Apply once daily to dry, clean scalp, and wash hands after application. Wash any non-treatment area that this medication may have accidentally come into contact with to avoid unwanted hair growth. Allow to dry thoroughly and wash off in the morning, or after at least 4 hours. Can be combined with other hair loss treatment products as directed by your healthcare provider.
What are the common side effects of Strut Hair Booster?
This medication is applied to the scalp. Report anything unusual to your healthcare provider. The most common side effects may include: Itching, burning, flaking, dryness, redness, swelling, scaling, hair color changes (usually darkening), scalp skin darkening, or irritation at the application site. In rare cases, chest pain or tightness, dizziness, headache, fast or irregular heartbeat, eye or vision changes, and muscle/joint/back pain may occur. See the Important Safety Information for a full list of potential side effects.
How to store Strut Hair Booster?
The Strut Hair Booster is made fresh in a pharmacy compounding laboratory, each time it is ordered. However, the active ingredient Latanoprost is stable for six (6) weeks when stored at room temperature. If you are not actively using Strut Hair Booster, store it in your refrigerator to preserve the dating. Once received, you have 6 weeks to use it. Store your Strut Hair Booster Formula closed in its original container. Store Strut Hair Booster Formula safely away from pets and children.
Can Strut Hair Booster be used if you are pregnant or breastfeeding?
Latanoprost has not been adequately studied in pregnancy. Strut Hair Booster should not be used if you are pregnant or breastfeeding unless your healthcare provider decides the benefit outweighs the risk. In general, we recommend that you exercise caution and do not use Strut Hair Booster during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
Can I travel with Strut Hair Booster?
Yes, you can use it while traveling, as long as it is within its expiration date. Strut Hair Booster is good for 6 weeks at room temperature.
What are the different formulation strengths available?
Strut Hair Booster comes in two strengths: Regular strength with 0.03% Latanoprost ($49 for a 30 ml, 30-day supply) and Max strength with 0.1% Latanoprost ($99 for a 30 ml, 30-day supply). Your provider will decide which strength is appropriate for you.
Can I order two bottles of Strut Hair Booster at a time for a multiple-month supply?
Since the Strut Hair Booster has a dating of 6 weeks, the pharmacy will only ship one 30ml bottle at a time, which is equivalent to a 30 day supply. You must pay for the individual bottle price each time. After 6 weeks we cannot confirm the stability or potency of the active ingredients.
