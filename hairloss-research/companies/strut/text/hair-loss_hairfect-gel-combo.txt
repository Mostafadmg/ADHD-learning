Frequently Asked Questions
How should I use Strut Men's Finasteride Hair Loss Formula?
Use the medicine exactly as directed. Using more medicine or applying it more often than prescribed will not make it work any faster, and may increase side effects. Do not take by mouth. Avoid getting this medication in your eyes or mouth, if this does occur, rinse with water. Apply once daily at night to dry, clean scalp, and wash hands after application. Wash any non-treatment area that this medication may have accidentally come into contact with to avoid unwanted hair growth. Allow to dry thoroughly and wash off in the morning, or after at least 4 hours.
What are the potential side effects of Strut Men's Compounded Topical Finasteride Formula?
Because this is a compounded topical medication, potential side effects depend on the ingredients in your prescribed formula. Application-site reactions may include itching, burning, dryness, flaking, scaling, or irritation. Finasteride applied to the skin can also be absorbed systemically, and adverse effects associated with finasteride have been reported with compounded topical products. Review the Important Safety Information for potential risks associated with your prescribed ingredients and contact your healthcare provider if you experience concerning symptoms.
How to store Strut Men's Finasteride Hair Loss Formula?
Store this medication in a cool, dry environment, protected from light, at room temperature (68F-77F). Always make sure the container is tightly closed when not using the medication. Keep all medications in a safe place out of reach of pets and children. Finasteride is in Strut Men's Finasteride Hair Loss Formula and is a category X medication, meaning that it can cause birth defects. For this reason, women who are currently or may become pregnant should not handle Strut Men's Finasteride Hair Loss Formula.
