How does Ketoconazole shampoo work?
Ketoconazole is considered an azole antifungal medication. It works by reducing fungus and yeast on areas where it is applied. Since an overgrowth of fungus or yeast on the scalp can be irritating or lead to dandruff development, keeping levels in check with a medicated shampoo may be helpful.
How should I use it?
Apply in the shower after you wet your hair, work into a lather focusing on your scalp, and leave on for 5 minutes before rinsing clean.
How should I store it?
Store in its closed original container at room temperature in a dry environment, safely away from pets and children.
Potential side effects
Most people will not experience side effects from using Ketoconazole shampoo. Infrequent effects include dryness, texture changes, itching, stinging, or irritation of the application site.
When may I see an effect?
You may be able to notice positive effects in as little as 2-3 weeks of use (results may vary).

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Ketoconazole shampoo?
Ketoconazole 2% shampoo is a prescription antifungal medication. It is FDA-approved to treat tinea (pityriasis) versicolor, a fungal skin infection. Clinicians may also prescribe ketoconazole shampoo off-label for certain scalp conditions, such as dandruff or seborrheic dermatitis, when medically appropriate.
How does Ketoconazole shampoo work?
Ketoconazole is an azole antifungal medication. It works by interfering with components of fungal cell membranes, which inhibits the growth of certain fungi and yeasts. Ketoconazole can reduce certain yeasts present on the scalp that may contribute to dandruff or seborrheic dermatitis.
How should I use Ketoconazole shampoo?
Use your Ketoconazole shampoo as directed by your healthcare provider and as written on your prescription and prescription information. For dandruff, the most common instructions are to use it every 3-4 days for up to 8 weeks to clear the fungus. You may be instructed to keep using Ketoconazole shampoo occasionally after this treatment phase to prevent dandruff recurrence. However, if you are using Ketoconazole shampoo for scalp inflammation, or for other reasons, you may be instructed to use it differently. Use Ketoconazole shampoo by applying it in the shower after you wet your hair. Put enough product in your hand to cover your scalp and clean your hair, then work into a lather focusing on your scalp and leave on for 5 minutes before rinsing clean. Afterward, continue your usual shower and hair regimen including conditioner or any other hair care products you normally use. Ketoconazole can be irritating to the eyes, so avoid getting the shampoo into the eyes or mouth and rinse thoroughly with water if it does contact these areas.
How to store Ketoconazole shampoo?
Store your Ketoconazole in its closed original container at room temperature in a dry environment. Store Ketoconazole shampoo safely away from pets and children.
What are the potential side effects of Ketoconazole shampoo?
Possible side effects of Ketoconazole 2% shampoo include scalp or skin irritation, itching, burning, dryness or oiliness, changes in hair texture, and application-site reactions. Hair discoloration and increased hair shedding have also been reported. Rare but serious allergic reactions, including severe swelling or difficulty breathing, have been reported. Stop using the medication and seek medical attention if you develop signs of a serious allergic reaction. See the Important Safety Information for additional risks and precautions.
How long until I may see an effect from Ketoconazole shampoo?
When prescribed off-label for dandruff or seborrheic dermatitis, improvement may occur gradually with treatment. Response time (some people have noticed change in as little as 2-3 weeks) varies depending on the condition being treated and your individual response.
What is the important safety information about this product?
Ketoconazole 2% shampoo is for topical use only. Avoid contact with the eyes and other mucous membranes. Do not use if you have a known allergy to Ketoconazole or any ingredient in the product. Stop treatment and contact your healthcare provider if significant irritation or an allergic reaction develops. Review the full Important Safety Information before use.
