Frequently Asked Questions
What is Oral Dutasteride?
Oral Dutasteride is a once-daily capsule available in two options: a 0.1mg capsule compounded with biotin, and a retail 0.5mg capsule with no biotin. Dutasteride is a 5-alpha reductase inhibitor that blocks both Type 1 and Type 2 enzymes responsible for converting testosterone into DHT, the hormone associated with male pattern hair loss.*
Do Dutasteride capsules always contain Biotin?
No. Two capsule options are available. The 0.1mg capsule is compounded with biotin. The 0.5mg capsule is a retail, manufactured product, so it is not compounded and does not contain biotin. You can state a preference during your online visit, and your Strut provider confirms which dose is appropriate for you.
How does Dutasteride work for hair loss?
Dutasteride may reduce DHT levels in the scalp by approximately 90% by blocking both isoforms of 5-alpha reductase. Lower DHT may slow follicle miniaturization and support healthier hair growth over time.*
How does Oral Dutasteride compare to Topical?
Oral Dutasteride works systemically and produces a higher level of DHT reduction. Topical Dutasteride acts more locally at the scalp and may reduce systemic exposure. Your Strut provider will help you decide which option fits your goals.
How long does Dutasteride take to show results?
It may take 3 to 6 months to begin seeing results, and up to 12 months for full results. Consistency is the most important variable.*
What happens if I stop taking Dutasteride?
DHT levels return to baseline over time after stopping, and the hair benefits achieved while on therapy will gradually be lost. Ongoing use is required to maintain results.
Is Dutasteride FDA-approved for hair loss?
Dutasteride is FDA-approved for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), not for hair loss. Use for hair loss is supervised by a licensed provider. Compounded Dutasteride is not FDA-approved.
Will Dutasteride affect my testosterone levels?
Dutasteride lowers DHT, not testosterone. Side effects can include decreased libido. See the safety information on this page, and discuss any concerns with your healthcare provider.
What are the side effects of Dutasteride?
Decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, decreased semen volume, breast tenderness or enlargement, and mood changes. See the safety information on this page.

Is shipping free?
Yes. Discreet & Free Shipping is included with every order, along with healthcare provider consultations and follow-ups.
What is the important safety information?
Dutasteride is for adult men only. It must not be handled by women who are or may become pregnant due to risk of fetal harm. Common side effects include changes in libido, erectile function, semen volume, breast tissue, and mood. Discuss your full medical history with your Strut provider before starting.
