Where is Hims & Hers available?
written by Theresa
Updated October 28, 2026 at 1:39 PM

Hims & Hers is currently available in all 50 states across the United States. However, it is important to note that specific treatments may be limited to certain states.

Please be aware that our platform is not currently accessible in Puerto Rico. 

Important Notes: 
For more information regarding our offerings in the United Kingdom, visit the following:
Hims: https://www.forhims.co.uk/
Hers: https://www.forhers.co.uk/
For more information regarding our offerings in Canada, visit the following: 
Hims: https://hims.ca/
Hers: https://forhers.ca/
For more information regarding our offerings in Australia, visit the following:
Hims: https://hims.com.au/
Hers: https://hers.com.au/
For more information regarding our offerings in Germany, visit the following: 
Hims: https://forhims.de/

 

If you have any additional questions, please don't hesitate to let us know! We're here for you.

Current and former subscribers: Log into your account and send us a message through the secure messaging portal.  

Not subscribed to a Hims or Hers plan but have a question: Click the "Contact us" button at the top right, and submit a question.
