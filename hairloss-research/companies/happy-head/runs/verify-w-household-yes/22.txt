Finasteride & Dutasteride: Partner Pregnancy Risk

Finasteride and Dutasteride are medications that may harm a developing male fetus if a pregnant person is exposed, even through skin contact. Extra precautions are required if your partner is pregnant or could become pregnant.

To reduce risk:

Your partner should not handle crushed or broken tablets or come into contact with the liquid form.
You must confirm your understanding of these risks if your partner is pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive.
