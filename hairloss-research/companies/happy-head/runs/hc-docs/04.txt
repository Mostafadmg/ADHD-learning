Happy Head Non-Prescription Products

Hair Serum
100% drug-free topical treatment
Physician-formulated to support healthy hair growth and reduce hair loss
Includes clinically studied actives and nourishing botanicals
Helps revitalize hair follicles
Promotes thicker, fuller-looking hair
Helps nourish the scalp
Anti-Gray Serum
Dermatologist-developed daily scalp serum
Supports the appearance of natural hair color
Helps improve the look of fuller, thicker hair
Promotes a healthier scalp environment
Formulated with peptides, melanin-support nutrients, and botanical extracts
Helps reduce the appearance of premature graying
Suitable for all hair types

FDA-approved and clinically proven hair regrowth treatment

Unscented, fast-absorbing, and easy to use

Over-the-counter and does not require a prescription

Helps revitalize thinning hair

Three 2 oz bottles

Over-the-counter and does not require a prescription

Designed to support hair growth and overall hair health
