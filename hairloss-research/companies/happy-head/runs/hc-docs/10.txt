How do I know if the SuperCapsule™ + ED Performance is right for me?
3 months ago Updated
About SuperCapsule™ + ED Performance

SuperCapsule™ + ED Performance is a prescription 3-in-1 daily capsule designed for men who want to address hair loss while also supporting sexual performance. It combines:

Dutasteride (0.5 mg) to help reduce DHT, a hormone linked to hair loss
Tadalafil (5 mg), the active ingredient in Cialis®, to support sexual performance and help offset potential sexual side effects associated with hair loss treatment
Vitamin D3 (2,000 IU) to support overall hair and skin health

This treatment may be right for you if you are looking for:

A once-daily prescription capsule instead of juggling multiple hair loss products
Support for hair regrowth and hair loss prevention
A treatment option that may help reduce concern about sexual side effects
A clinician-reviewed prescription delivered directly to your door

You may be a good candidate if you:

Are experiencing hair thinning or hair loss
Want a treatment that combines hair loss support and ED-performance support in one formula
Prefer a simple daily routine
Are comfortable completing an online consultation for clinician review
Have been determined by a licensed clinician to be an appropriate fit for this prescription treatment
Next steps

To get started, complete the online intake so a licensed clinician can review your health history and determine whether SuperCapsule™ + ED Performance is safe and appropriate for you. If approved, your treatment will be shipped directly to you.
