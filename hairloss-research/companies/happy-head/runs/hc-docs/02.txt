Which of your hair growth solutions are customizable?
1 year ago Updated

Most of our topical solutions are custom-made to your needs, and are appropriately labeled with the 'custom' tag on our product page. But there are a few specialized formulas that cannot be modified. Here are the topical solutions that are not customizable:

- Topical Latanoprost, Finasteride, Minoxidil

- Topical Finasteride & Minoxidil (Aqueous Formula)

- Topical Finasteride SuperSolution (Liposomal Formula)

- TopicalRx Advanced ProBlend

- TopicalRx SuperSolution™ ProBlend

- TopicalRx Beard Formula

Please note oral medications are not customizable; the listed concentrations on the respective product pages are the only dosages we can provide. For topical products, reach out to customer support for the full range of customization options.

 

Note: Most of our topical ingredients are customizable, however, there are some base options that are not. The customizable formulas we offer are noted on our products page here.
