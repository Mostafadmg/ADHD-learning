Does Happy Head accept outside insurance, FSA, or HSA?
3 months ago Updated

Happy Head does not accept outside insurance. Our treatments are categorized as cosmetic, and cosmetic treatments are generally not covered by insurance or HSA/FSA plans.

Customers may try using an HSA or FSA card at checkout, but acceptance is not guaranteed and depends on the card provider.

If a customer needs documentation for FSA or HSA reimbursement, they can request a receipt or invoice for their order or prescription from their assigned Happy Head dermatologist.

We understand this may be disappointing and apologize for the inconvenience. If you have any additional questions, please reach out to our support team. We are happy to help!
