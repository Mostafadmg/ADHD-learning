Should I choose a SuperCapsule™ or topical treatment?
2 months ago Updated

Choosing between a SuperCapsule™ and a topical treatment depends on your preferences, medical history, and whether oral medication is appropriate for you. Both options are designed to support hair growth, but they differ in how often they are used and their potential risk of side effects.

 
SuperCapsule™ vs. topical treatment
Feature	SuperCapsule™	Topical treatment
How it is used	Taken once daily	Usually applied with a dropper twice daily for best results
Convenience	One daily dose	Requires a regular topical application routine
Side-effect risk	May have a higher risk of systemic side effects because it is taken orally	May have a lower risk of systemic side effects
Formulation	Available as a 3-in-1 capsule	Can be customized to meet your individual needs
Expected results	With consistent use, may provide results similar to topical treatment	With consistent use, may provide results similar to oral treatment

Topical applications may also be available for people who prefer a once-daily routine. Follow the application instructions provided with your prescription.

 
SuperCapsule™ options

The SuperCapsule™ is a once-daily, 3-in-1 hair-growth capsule available in the following formulations:

Finasteride, Minoxidil, and Vitamin D
Dutasteride, minoxidil, and Vitamin D
Spironolactone, Minoxidil, and Vitamin D — for women under 50
Which option is right for you?

A SuperCapsule™ may be a better fit if you prefer taking one capsule per day. A topical treatment may be preferable if you want to minimize the potential for systemic side effects or prefer not to take oral medication.

 

Your provider can help determine which option is appropriate based on your medical history and treatment goals. Use your selected treatment consistently as directed for the best chance of seeing results.
