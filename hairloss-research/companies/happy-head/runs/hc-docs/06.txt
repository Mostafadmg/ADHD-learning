Why do you need my photos?
1 year ago Updated

A consultation with one of our board-certified dermatologists is the first step to starting your hair growth journey with Happy Head. For this reason, it may be a requirement to submit a picture(s) of your scalp/hairline, a picture of your ID, and/or a 5-second video for patient verification purposes if prompted. 

We take your healthcare very seriously, so our doctors are required to verify your identity in order to issue a medically responsible diagnosis. We understand that there are security concerns. Happy Head uses industry-standard security to keep all of your private information secure. 

Here’s a few ways you can submit your photos and/or video (all channels are HIPAA compliant):  

1. Upload to your account page here

2. Submit here

3. Text 213-464-0214

Once this step is completed, your assigned dermatologist will contact you via email and send your order to the pharmacy.
