What is your return policy?
2 months ago Updated

We want you to feel confident in your purchase.

Prescription products
If you are not satisfied with your most recent prescription order, you may request either:
a complimentary reformulation, pending the dermatologist’s recommendation, or
a refund if you choose to cancel.
To receive a refund, the newest bottle(1) must be returned within 30 days of delivery.
The product must be returned in its original packaging.
Customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping.
If the return is approved, the refund will cover the full purchase amount excluding return shipping costs.
Non-prescription products

We do not accept returns for non-prescription items, including:

Shampoo
Conditioner
Hair serum
Scalp dermaroller
2% Minoxidil Topical Solution, USP (Women’s Formula)
5% Minoxidil Topical Solution, USP (Men’s Formula)
Anti-Gray Serum
Hair Growth Supplements
Reformulation Option

If you are unsatisfied with your prescription treatment, you may be eligible for a complimentary reformulation.

Reformulations are available pending the dermatologist’s recommendation.
This option may be offered instead of a return, depending on your treatment needs and medical review.
Subscription Management

You can manage your subscription at any time through your account settings.

You may adjust your monthly subscription directly in your account.
To prevent any further charges, you must contact customer support directly to request cancellation.
How to Get Help

To request a return, ask about a reformulation, or cancel your subscription, please contact customer support directly.
