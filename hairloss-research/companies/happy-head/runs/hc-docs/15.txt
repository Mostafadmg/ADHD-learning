Why Happy Head Asks for Your Age and Gender
3 months ago Updated

Happy Head asks for your age and gender during the ordering process to help ensure your prescription is safe, appropriate, and legally compliant.

This information helps our licensed healthcare professionals determine which treatments may be right for you.

 
Why We Ask for Your Age

We ask for your age for the following reasons:

Prescription medications are only available to adults age 18 and older
Legal and medical guidelines require us to verify age before prescribing
Formulation requirements may vary by age group
Some treatment options may differ for individuals under 50 and 50 or older
Age may affect your eligibility for certain medications
 
Why We Ask for Your Gender

We ask for your gender to help match you with the most appropriate treatment options.

This information is important because:

Some ingredients may not be suitable for everyone
Certain formulations include gender-specific considerations
Your tele-health questionnaire responses help determine which options may be appropriate for you
Gender may affect your eligibility for some prescription formulations
 
How We Determine Your Prescription

Your prescription is based on:

Your age
Your gender
Your tele-health questionnaire responses
Your individual needs
The clinical judgment of our licensed healthcare professionals
 
Important to Know

Not every customer is eligible for every treatment option.

Your final prescription, if approved, will be customized based on your information and your provider’s recommendation.
