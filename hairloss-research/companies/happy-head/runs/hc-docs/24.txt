What Is Dutasteride for Hair Loss?
1 month ago Updated

Dutasteride is a prescription hair loss medication that works by blocking DHT (dihydrotestosterone), the hormone commonly associated with hair loss. Dutasteride is considered stronger than Finasteride because it blocks DHT more extensively and remains in the body longer.

 

Because of its potency, Dutasteride may provide enhanced hair growth results for some patients, although individual results will vary.

 

Dutasteride is generally recommended for:

Men ages 18 and older
Women over age 50, when determined appropriate by a healthcare provider
How Long Does Dutasteride Take to Work?

Patients may begin noticing improvement in hair growth within approximately 3 to 6 months of consistent use, though timelines and outcomes vary by individual.

What Is the Difference Between Dutasteride and Finasteride?

Both medications are DHT blockers used to treat hair loss, but there are important differences:

Finasteride is commonly prescribed as a first-line treatment for hair loss.
Dutasteride blocks DHT 3 times stronger than Finasteride.
Dutasteride remains in the body longer than Finasteride.
Some patients may experience greater hair regrowth results with Dutasteride.

The best treatment option depends on factors such as medical history, treatment goals, and provider recommendations.
