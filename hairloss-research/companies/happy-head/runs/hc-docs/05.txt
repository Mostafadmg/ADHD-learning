What is the ordering process for Happy Head?
2 months ago Updated
Online Ordering Process
Visit the product page and select the treatment you want to order.
Complete the telehealth questionnaire with your age, gender, and medical information for the dermatologist’s review.
After checkout, upload photos of your hair for evaluation. In some cases, you may also be asked to upload a photo ID for verification.
A board-certified dermatologist will review your information and contact you by email if needed to confirm your prescription or provide recommendations.
Once approved, the pharmacy will prepare your treatment and ship it directly to your address.
Subscription Information

Orders are processed as part of a subscription service with recurring monthly shipments. You can cancel your subscription at any time.

 
Ordering by Phone

If you prefer to place an order by phone, call 844-962-4247 to speak with a hair specialist.

Phone support hours:

Monday–Friday: 6 AM – 5 PM PST
Weekends: 9 AM – 5 PM PST
