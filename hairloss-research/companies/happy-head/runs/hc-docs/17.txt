What is TopicalRx SuperSolution™ ProBlend
7 months ago Updated

Happy Head offers a cutting-edge hair regrowth treatment with Topical Rx SuperSolution™, combining the strongest FDA-approved ingredients like Minoxidil, Dutasteride, and Finasteride in a lipodermal base for optimal absorption and reduced side effects. Applied once daily with 5 mess-free pumps, this 10-in-1 formula targets hair loss directly at the follicles.

 

The formula includes ingredients such as Minoxidil, Cetirizine HCl, Latanoprost, Dutasteride, Finasteride, Melatonin, Caffeine, Tretinoin, Vitamin D3, and Biotin, each targeting hair growth and scalp health. Benefit from free dermatologist consultations and customized prescriptions. You can upgrade with 2.5% Ketoconazole for anti-dandruff benefits.

 

Each component targets a specific factor in hair growth, from hormonal regulation and improved blood flow to enhanced nutrient delivery and scalp health. Designed for comprehensive hair restoration, this treatment helps optimize growth, thickness, and overall hair quality. This approach provides a gradual release of the medication to maximizing growth potential.

 

Most users can expect to see visible results in hair regrowth and improved hair density within 3- 6 months. The combination of FDA-approved ingredients like Minoxidil and Dutasteride works to stimulate hair follicles, prolong the growth phase, and block DHT (a common cause of hair loss). Consistent daily application is key during this period.
