What if I don't want a prescription?
3 months ago Updated

If you’re not interested in prescription hair growth treatments, we completely understand. We want you to feel comfortable and confident in your hair growth journey.

For customers who prefer non-prescription options, we offer a range of over-the-counter and 100% drug-free products that can support thicker-, healthier-looking hair.

 
100% Drug-Free Products

Our 100% drug-free hair care options include:

Thickening Shampoo
Thickening Conditioner
Hair Serum
Anti-Gray Serum
Hair Growth Supplements

These products are designed for customers who want a non-prescription approach to hair and scalp care.

 
Over-the-Counter Minoxidil Options

If you’re looking for an OTC hair growth treatment that does not require a prescription, we offer:

5% Minoxidil Topical Solution, USP (Men’s Formula)
2% Minoxidil Topical Solution, USP (Women’s Formula)

These options are available without a prescription and may help stimulate hair growth.

 
Prescription Hair Growth Medications

If you decide you want a prescription option, we also specialize in custom hair growth medications with high-strength ingredient concentrations.

Customers who choose prescription treatment are paired with a board-certified dermatologist who can:

Answer questions about treatment
Review your needs
Adjust your formulation as needed
 
Need More Help?

If you have questions about the process or want help choosing the right option, please contact our support team.
