How can I cancel my subscription?
2 months ago Updated

You can cancel your Happy Head subscription at any time by phone or email.

Cancel by Phone
Call (844) 962-4247.
Business hours:
Monday–Friday: 6:00 AM–5:00 PM PST
Weekends: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM PST
Cancel by Email
Send your cancellation request to help@happyhead.com.
Include the email address associated with your account so we can locate your subscription.
Important Information About Existing Orders
Submitting a cancellation request does not cancel existing orders.
We cannot reverse or stop an order after it has been marked Processing and submitted to the pharmacy.
