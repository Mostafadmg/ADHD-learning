TopicalRx Dandruff Solution: Clobetasol + Ketoconazole by Happy Head
2 months ago Updated

Dandruff and scalp irritation can be ongoing and uncomfortable. Happy Head offers a prescription-strength treatment designed by dermatologists to help reduce itching, flaking, redness, and scalp discomfort.

This treatment combines two active ingredients that work together to improve scalp health and reduce dandruff at the source.

What Is the Happy Head Dandruff Solution?

Happy Head’s dandruff treatment is a prescription-only solution created to help manage:

Flaking
Itching
Redness
Scalp irritation
Dry scalp discomfort

The treatment includes two active ingredients:

Clobetasol 0.05%

Clobetasol helps reduce scalp inflammation and irritation. It can help calm redness, itching, and discomfort associated with stubborn dandruff and scalp conditions.

Ketoconazole

Ketoconazole is an antifungal ingredient that helps reduce the fungus commonly linked to dandruff. It also helps improve overall scalp health and reduce irritation.

The treatment includes:

Ketoconazole 2.5% in the topical solution
Ketoconazole 2% in the shampoo
What’s Included in the Treatment Plan?

Your subscription includes:

1 bottle of topical solution

Ketoconazole 2.5%
2 bottles of Ketoconazole 2% shampoo
Dermatologist consultation and treatment oversight
Personalized treatment recommendations

How to Use the Products
Topical Solution

Use the Clobetasol + Ketoconazole solution as directed:

Apply 1 mL directly to the scalp
Use twice daily:
Morning
Night
Apply every other week or as instructed by your dermatologist
Ketoconazole Shampoo
Use every other day
Apply to wet hair and scalp
Leave on for 3–5 minutes
Rinse thoroughly
When to Expect Results

Many users begin noticing improvement within 2–3 weeks of consistent use.

Common improvements may include:

Less itching
Reduced flaking
Calmer scalp skin
Healthier-looking scalp and hair

Results may vary depending on the individual and consistency of use.

Safety and Side Effects

Most people tolerate the treatment well when used as directed.

Possible side effects may include:

Mild dryness
Temporary scalp irritation
Sensitivity at the application site

Because this is a prescription-strength treatment:

A prescription is required
Treatment is supervised by a licensed dermatologist
Regular follow-ups may be recommended to monitor progress and safety

Always use the medication exactly as prescribed.

 
Frequently Asked Questions
What makes this treatment effective for dandruff?

The combination of Clobetasol and Ketoconazole helps target both scalp irritation and the fungal causes commonly associated with dandruff.

How long does it take to see results?

Many users report improvement within 2–3 weeks when using the treatment consistently.

Are there side effects?

Most users do not experience major side effects. Some people may notice mild dryness or irritation. Your dermatologist can adjust your treatment plan if needed.

Conclusion

Happy Head’s prescription-strength dandruff treatment is designed to help people manage persistent scalp irritation, itching, and flaking with dermatologist-guided care.

By combining anti-inflammatory and antifungal ingredients into one treatment plan, the solution supports healthier scalp conditions while offering flexible subscription management and ongoing medical support.
