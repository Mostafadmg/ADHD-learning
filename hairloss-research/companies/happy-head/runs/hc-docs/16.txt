What is Topical Rx Advanced ProBlend?
3 months ago Updated

Topical Rx Advanced ProBlend is a prescription-based, dermatologist-prescribed topical treatment designed to target hair loss at its root cause. This advanced 10-in-1 formula combines FDA-approved ingredients commonly used to support hair regrowth, reduce shedding, and improve overall scalp and follicle health.

It is formulated for men ages 18+ and women ages 50+.

 
What Topical Rx Advanced ProBlend Helps With

Topical Rx Advanced ProBlend is designed to:

Block DHT (dihydrotestosterone), a major contributor to hair loss
Support hair regrowth in thinning areas
Extend the anagen (growth) phase of the hair cycle
Increase hair density
Stimulate healthy follicle activity
Reduce inflammation around hair follicles
Support a healthier scalp environment for ongoing growth
 
Key Ingredients

The formula includes the following active ingredients:

8% Minoxidil
1% Cetirizine HCl
0.25% Dutasteride
0.25% Finasteride
0.2% Biotin
0.2% Caffeine
0.005% Latanoprost
0.001% Tretinoin
1,000 IU/mL Vitamin D3
How These Ingredients Work

These ingredients are selected to address multiple causes of hair thinning and loss:

Minoxidil helps stimulate hair regrowth
Cetirizine HCl helps reduce inflammation around hair follicles
Dutasteride and Finasteride help block DHT
Latanoprost may help increase hair density and support follicle activity
Caffeine helps stimulate scalp circulation
Tretinoin promotes cell turnover and may help improve absorption of Minoxidil
Biotin supports keratin production and hair strength
Vitamin D3 supports scalp and follicle health
How to Apply

Each bottle contains 50 mL (1.7 oz) of solution and includes a calibrated dropper for more precise application.

 

Use the product as follows unless your provider instructs otherwise:

Fill the dropper twice to 0.5 mL
Apply directly to the scalp for a total of 1.0 mL per application
Gently rub the solution into the scalp
Apply twice daily, once in the morning and once at night
Total daily use is 2.0 mL

For best results, use the treatment consistently and take monthly progress photos to track changes over time.

 
When to Expect Results

With consistent use, many patients may begin to see visible results within 3 to 6 months. Results vary by individual and are not guaranteed.
