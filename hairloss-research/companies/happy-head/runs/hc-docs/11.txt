Subscription vs. One-Time Orders
2 months ago Updated

We offer both subscription and one-time purchase options for prescription and non-prescription products. If you prefer to purchase only when needed, one-time order options are available directly on each product page.

 
Subscriptions

For many customers, a subscription is the most convenient way to keep orders on schedule. Subscriptions are designed to simplify ongoing treatment and reduce interruptions in care.

With a subscription, you can:

Receive products on a recurring schedule
Avoid repeating the consultation process for ongoing prescription refills
Potentially save time and money over the course of treatment

You can manage your subscription at any time by logging into your account and selecting the Subscriptions tab.

 
One-Time Orders

One-time orders are available for customers who prefer more flexibility and want to purchase only when needed.

However, there are some important limitations for prescription one-time orders:

Customized topical formulations are not available
You must choose from the standard options listed on the product page
Each prescription one-time order requires completion of the initial consultation process to confirm the treatment is still appropriate for you
 
Summary

Subscriptions are designed to make ongoing treatment management simpler and more convenient. One-time orders are available for customers who prefer flexibility, but prescription one-time orders require a new consultation each time and do not include customized topical formulations.
