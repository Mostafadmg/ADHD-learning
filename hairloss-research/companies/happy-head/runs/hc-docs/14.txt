Do you ship internationally?
9 months ago Updated

Unfortunately, we do not ship internationally. Happy Head only operates and ships within the USA at this time. Our doctors are licensed to prescribe medication exclusively within the USA, and we must adhere to these regulations to ensure compliance and patient safety.
