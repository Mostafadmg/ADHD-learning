About Happy Head's pricing
2 months ago Updated

Happy Head offers personalized hair loss treatments formulated and prescribed based on each patient’s needs. Treatment costs depend on the medication, ingredients, dosage, and shipment frequency selected.

Monthly Treatment Pricing

Happy Head custom-compounded prescriptions generally cost $59–$119 per month. Some individual treatments or product combinations may fall outside this range.

The final monthly price depends on:

The active ingredients in the prescription
Whether the treatment is topical, oral, or a combination
The prescribed strength and dosage
The selected treatment plan
Whether multiple products are purchased together
Typical Pricing Examples
Treatment type	Typical monthly price
Custom-compounded prescription	$24–$119
Oral or topical treatment plans	Price varies by formulation
Combination treatment plans	May cost more than a single treatment
Multi-month purchases	May qualify for savings

Prices and product availability may change. The price displayed during checkout is the applicable price for the selected treatment plan.

What the Price Includes

Depending on the treatment plan, the listed price may include:

An online consultation with a board-certified dermatologist
A personalized treatment recommendation
Custom-compounded medication
Complimentary shipping
Ongoing treatment support
Access to a dedicated care manager

There are no separate consultation or standard shipping charges for eligible orders. Additional charges may apply if a patient chooses optional products or services.

Why Treatment Prices Vary
Board-Certified Dermatologist Review

A board-certified dermatologist reviews the patient’s information and determines whether prescription treatment is appropriate. The dermatologist may recommend a specific ingredient, dosage, or combination based on the patient’s hair loss pattern, age, medical history, and treatment goals.

Personalized Formulations

Happy Head prescriptions are customized according to the treatment plan rather than using one identical formula for every patient. Customized ingredients may include minoxidil, finasteride, dutasteride, latanoprost, retinoic acid, or other medications when clinically appropriate.

Fresh Compounding

Eligible prescriptions are prepared for the individual treatment plan. The formulation and dosage may affect the final price.

Ongoing Support

Treatment pricing may include continued support during the treatment process, including help with order questions, treatment instructions, and general care coordination.

Discounts and Multi-Month Savings

Happy Head may offer promotional pricing or savings on eligible multi-month purchases. The amount of savings depends on the product, treatment plan, promotion, and purchase quantity.

Before placing an order, review the checkout summary for:

The price per shipment
The billing frequency
Any promotional discount
The number of treatment months included
The renewal price after the promotion ends

Promotional offers may change or expire without notice. A discount available for one product or treatment plan may not apply to another.

Important Pricing Information
The $24–$119 monthly range applies to many custom-compounded prescription formulas but is not a guarantee for every treatment.
Combination treatments and optional products may increase the total cost.
Prescription eligibility is determined by a licensed medical professional.
Treatment results vary from person to person.
A prescription treatment may not be appropriate for every patient.
Always review the complete order total and renewal terms before purchasing.
Pricing Summary

Happy Head personalized hair loss treatments typically cost $24–$119 per month, depending on the prescribed ingredients and treatment plan. The listed price may include the dermatologist consultation, personalized treatment plan, medication, shipping, and ongoing support. Multi-month purchases and limited-time promotions may reduce the effective monthly cost, but discounts and product prices are subject to change.
