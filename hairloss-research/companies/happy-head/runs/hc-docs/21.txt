How can I make updates or customize my prescription medication?
7 months ago Updated

Happy Head offers customization for many prescription formulas, including most topical treatments. If you are a new customer, you can request changes to your formula during the ordering process. To get started, select your desired product on the Happy Head products page or click Start Now. As part of checkout, you will complete a tele-health questionnaire that includes a dedicated field for customization requests. This is where you can ask to adjust the strength of active ingredients, request specific ingredients, or exclude ingredients due to sensitivities or allergies.

 

After you place your order, your assigned board-certified dermatologist will review your medical history and any customization requests to determine the best formula for you. If you have additional questions or would like to discuss your preferences in more detail, you will also have the opportunity to communicate directly with your dermatologist during your consultation. Once your prescription is confirmed, the pharmacy will compound your customized formula and ship it directly to your door.

 

If you choose a one-time order, please note that one-time orders for topical and oral prescriptions are not eligible for customization. In that case, you will need to choose from the standard formula options listed on the product page.

 

If you are an existing customer and would like to request an update to your prescription, you can contact Happy Head through the Help Center. You may also send a private message to your Happy Head dermatologist through the patient portal to ask about adjustments to your formula.

 

Please note that most topical ingredients are customizable, but some base formula options cannot be changed. The products that support customization are identified on the Happy Head products page.
