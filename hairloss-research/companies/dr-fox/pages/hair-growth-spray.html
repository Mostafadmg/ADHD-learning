About Dr Fox Dual Action Hair Growth Spray

  • Dr Fox Dual Action Hair Growth Spray reduces male pattern hair loss and stimulates new hair growth, resulting in thicker, longer hairs, and more visible hair coverage.
  • The spray contains finasteride 0.3%, minoxidil 5%, and tretinoin 0.01%.
  • Apply once daily to the scalp (suggest at night time).
  • Supplied on prescription as an unlicensed 'special' medicine.
  • Reduced application of 6 sprays (1ml) daily.
  • Read the patient leaflet for instructions, possible side effects, cautions for use, and potential interactions with other medicines.

Who can use Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray

  • Adults (aged 18+).
  • Assigned male at birth.
  • Affected by male 'pattern' hair loss.
  • Read the warning and cautions before using.

How to use Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray

  • Wash and dry your scalp.
  • Apply 6 pumps of product (equivalent to 1ml) to the scalp daily (suggest at night time).
  • Rub in gently then wash your hands thoroughly.
  • Avoid breathing the spray in during application.

Most beneficial stages of hair loss to use the Hair Growth Spray

When the spray is started early enough in the hair loss process and is used continually, hair thinning/excess hair loss can be prevented. The treatment works best at the mild-to moderate stages of hair loss: stages 2-5 of the Hamilton-Norwood scale (see illustration below).

Stages of hair loss information

  1. No notable hair loss or recession of the hairline.
  2. Often referred to as the 'adult' or 'mature' hairline: some recession at the temples and some sparsity at the front-centre.
  3. Two variants:
    1. Either development of deeper recession at the temples with increased sparsity at the front-centre.
    2. Or recession at the temples and front-centre remains at stage 2, but there is notable hair loss/sparsity at the crown (vertex).
  4. Recession at the temples and front-centre is developed beyond that of stage 2 and there is hair loss/sparsity at the crown.
  5. Recession at the temples and front-centre hairlines is developed beyond stage 4 along with increased hair loss/sparsity at the crown. The band of hair between these areas is still present but sparse.
  6. The areas of hair loss at the front, temples, and crown have extended and merged: the band of hair crossing the crown is lost, with only sparse hair remaining.
  7. The remaining hair is an (often sparse) band around the back of the head connecting the remaining hair at the sides.

When can I expect to see results?

Hair on the scalp grows at approximately 1cm per month. Thicker, longer, hair with greater scalp coverage, may be seen as early as 3 months with daily use. Excess hair loss has usually stopped after 6 months of daily use and changes are more obvious, with maximum benefit by 1-2 years.

In the unlikely event your hair loss continues beyond 12 months treatment, with no notable increase in hair thickness or coverage, you are likely to be a non-responder and it is unlikely you will benefit from continuing treatment.

Once improved hair coverage has been achieved, continued treatment is required to maintain the benefits. If treatment is stopped, pre-treatment appearance will likely return within 3 to 6 months.

How Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray works

In the average adult male approximately 10% of the male sex hormone testosterone is converted to the more potent hormone Dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT is responsible for adult male characteristics including male hair thinning and hair loss. DHT affects hair follicles in a way that prevents hairs from reaching their full growth potential.

For more information see Dihydrotestosterone (Society for Endocrinology).

The active ingredients in the Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray work to reverse hair thinning and hair loss using a combination of the following treatments:

Finasteride

Finasteride reduces the conversion of testosterone to DHT, therefore less DHT reaches hair follicles and hair is allowed to reach its full growth potential.

For more information see Common questions about finasteride (NHS).

Finasteride has traditionally been taken in tablet form (orally) and 1mg finasteride is licensed for use to treat male pattern hair loss. Clinical trials have shown topical finasteride has comparable results with oral finasteride from doses of 0.5mg to 5mg daily and may have fewer side effects than oral treatment however is not yet licensed.

Minoxidil

Minoxidil relaxes small blood vessels where it is applied to the scalp, increasing blood flow to hair follicles. Blood carries essential oxygen and nutrients which stimulate new hair growth, encouraging thicker, longer, more visible hair. Minoxidil has been proven to stimulate hair regrowth, likely by enhancing cell multiplication.

Topical minoxidil is already licensed to treat male pattern hair loss (e.g. as Regaine).

Tretinoin

Tretinoin is a vitamin-A derivative that promotes hair growth indirectly by increasing minoxidil uptake. Minoxidil spray alone is usually used twice daily, but combined with tretinoin allows minoxidil to be used once daily with equivalent results in clinical trials.

Other ingredients

In addition to minoxidil, finasteride, and tretinoin, the spray contains glycerol, sulphuric acid, industrial methylated spirit, and water. These ingredients are commonly used in topical formulations and help deliver the active medications effectively to the scalp.

DO NOT use Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray if you:

  • Have an allergy or hypersensitivity to finasteride, minoxidil, or tretinoin.
  • Are susceptible to severe scalp infections or psoriasis.
  • May have hair loss due to a cause other than hereditary 'pattern' hair loss, e.g. chemotherapy, total alopecia (hair loss over the entire body). If you are unsure you should consult your GP.
  • Were assigned female at birth.

Cautions for use of Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray

  • Keep away from children and animals. If swallowed, minoxidil can lead to heart toxicity.
  • Pregnant women and women with childbearing potential should avoid using or handling as finasteride may affect development of a baby's genitals.
  • Inform your doctor if you have a PSA (prostate specific antigen) blood test, as topical finasteride may alter the test result.
  • Avoid exposure to UV light (sunlight) as tretinoin can make the skin more likely to burn.
  • If treatment is stopped the beneficial effects will likely reverse within 6 months.

Consult your GP before using if you:

  • Have heart disease, treated or untreated high or low blood pressure (cautions for minoxidil 5%).
  • Have a history of urinary obstruction, or difficulty passing urine, this may be caused by an enlarged prostate gland (caution for finasteride).
  • Have any male breast changes: growth of breast tissue (gynaecomastia), lumps, pain, enlargement, or nipple discharge.

Possible side effects of Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray

Side effects may be less likely when using topical rather than tablet (oral) medication as active ingredients are delivered directly to the scalp with a smaller amount entering into the bloodstream.

Skin irritation (itching, dryness, redness) at the application site can occur and is most likely due to the alcohol base, but may be due to minoxidil or tretinoin. Using a scalp oil such as jojoba or coconut oil, or a lightweight, non-oil-based scalp serum, can help reduce these effects. If you develop bothersome skin redness, rash, or other irritation, stop treatment. If irritation is severe or does not improve after stopping treatment, you should seek advice from a pharmacist or your GP.

Stop treatment and seek advice from your GP if you experience:

  • Bothersome dizziness or feeling faint which persists after stopping treatment.
  • More rapid weight gain, or weight gain associated with breathlessness.
  • Persistent, bothersome nausea.
  • Low mood, depression or feeling depressed (feelings of sadness and unworthiness).
  • Any change in breast tissue.

Rare sexual side effects:

  • Reduced male fertility - advise stop treatment 3 months prior to planned conception.
  • Reduced libido and erectile dysfunction.

Please stop treatment and seek advice from your GP urgently if you experience:

  • Chest pain and/or palpitations (an uncomfortable awareness of your heart beating in your chest).
  • Severe depression/suicidal thoughts.
  • An allergic reaction: swelling of the lips, face, tongue, or throat; breathing difficulties or difficulty swallowing.

For the full list of potential side effects read the Patient Information Leaflet (PIL).

Reporting side effects

Worrying or unwanted side effects should be reported via the MHRAYellow Card scheme, also available via their app which can be downloaded from Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

Please keep your regular GP informed. You can also contact Dr Fox doctors if you need medical advice about the product.

Alternative hair loss treatments

Oral finasteride 1mg tablets and minoxidil only topical applications are also available from Dr Fox. These should not be used at the same time as the Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray.

Microneedling, hair transplants, platelet-rich plasma injections, and low level laser therapy are other alternative hair loss treatments.

The hair growth cycle

Hair growth is a continuous process which can be explained via 4 cycling phases:

  1. Anagen (growth) - a single shaft of hair grows from a hair follicle for 2-8 years. Hair length depends upon the length of this phase (unless the hair is cut).
  2. Catagen (regression) - over 1-2 weeks, the supporting hair follicle regresess and detaches from the hair. The hair however remains in place, secured by a small white bulb of keratin.
  3. Telogen (resting) - the old hair rests for 2-3 months. while the old hair is resting, a new hair develops at the base of the hair follicle (the start of anagen for the new hair).
  4. Exogen/Anogen restart (shedding) - as the new hair grows, it pushes the old hair out. On the diagram above, exogen occurs between anagen restart and anagen.

For individuals not experiencing hair loss, it is normal to shed over 100 scalp hairs per day!

  • If the anagen/growth phase moves into the catagen/regression phase prematurely, excess hair shedding and thinning occurs. This is characteristic of male pattern hair loss.
  • If the percentage of hair follicles remaining in the telogen/resting phase is increased, hair loss decreases.

Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray FAQs

Why should I choose Dr Fox Dual Action Hair Growth Spray?

Dr Fox hair growth spray contains both finasteride and minoxidil, with tretinoin to boost their effects and allow once daily use. It is a great option combining 2 proven treatments for male pattern hair loss in one application.

Can I use the Hair Growth Spray at the same time as finasteride tablets?

No. The Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray contains finasteride and should not be used at the same time as taking oral finasteride.

Is Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray licensed to treat male hair loss?

Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray is manufactured as a 'specials' medicine by a licensed UK medicine manufacturer, Xeal Pharma (MS 46688).

Specials medicines are not required to go through the medicines licensing process.

Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray is supplied on prescription only and patients must consent to being supplied with an unlicensed medicine during the consultation and ordering process.

Both finasteride and minoxidil are proven to help treat male pattern hair loss.

Can finasteride cause sexual problems?

There is much controversy online about this subject that is associated with oral tablets. The manufacturer's finasteride 1mg tablet patient information leaflet provides details and the NHS reviewed a study regarding these concerns in 2017 and concluded that stopping the tablets would resolve sexual problems in nearly all cases. See also the mystery of post-finasteride syndrome.

As a topical application, the Dr Fox Hair Growth Spray is applied to the scalp only, and only small amounts of the active ingredients will pass into the blood stream. This may reduce the risk of side effects.

See also FAQs: Hair loss treatment.
