## 
What tablets are part of the ‘Dual Action’ bundle? 
This combination pairs a licensed tablet for male pattern hair loss (finasteride) with a second tablet treatment used off-label for hair loss. Off-label means the medicine is licensed in the UK, but its licence does not specifically cover its use for hair loss.  A clinician, after reviewing evidence including the medical background of the patient and their own clinical judgement, may prescribe it when they consider it clinically appropriate. 
This bundle is intended to treat patients with male pattern hair loss in two different ways. 
Patients cannot access this bundle without a successful consultation reviewed by a clinician. A clinician will assess whether treatment is clinically appropriate. Completing a consultation does not guarantee that treatment will be prescribed 
Individual results vary, and not everyone responds to treatment. Your clinician can talk you through the reasoning behind recommending this combination for you specifically. 
## How does treatment work? 
Finasteride reduces the conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone, or DHT. By lowering the levels of DHT in the scalp, this may reduce further hair loss and support hair regrowth in some men. 
Find out more information on the finasteride [A:https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/files/pil.7068.pdf] patient information leaflet. 
The second tablet is prescribed off-label for hair loss. It affects blood vessels and may help support hair growth.
## How do you take each treatment? 
Each tablet should be taken once daily around the same time, with or without food. If you miss a dose, take the next dose at the usual time. Do not take a double dose. 
Always take each medicine exactly as prescribed. Your clinician may give you different instructions based on your individual circumstances. 
Treatment usually needs to be continued to maintain its effect, but only continue for as long as your clinician advises. Seek advice promptly if you experience side effects. 
## How long does the hair loss treatment take to work? 
The medicines begin acting in the body after treatment starts, but visible improvement may take several months and is not guaranteed. Your clinician will advise when treatment should be reviewed, which may be around 6 to 12 months after starting.  
Continued treatment is required to maintain any benefits. If treatment is stopped, hair loss will generally begin to return over time.
## 
Side effects of the Dual Action tablets 
The possible side effects depend on which medicine is prescribed. Your clinician will explain the relevant risks, and you will receive information for each medicine before starting treatment.
#### 
Finasteride side effects 
Uncommon (up to 1 in 100 people):  
difficulty getting or keeping an erection  
reduced desire for sex  
changes in ejaculation, for example a decrease in the amount of semen released 
Not known:  
breast swelling or tenderness  
testicular pain  
blood in semen  
palpitations  
persistent erectile, libido, or ejaculation problems after stopping treatment  
male infertility and/or poor semen quality  
changes in liver function shown on a blood test  
anxiety  
suicidal thoughts

increased heart rate  
inflammation of the lining that surrounds the heart

increased hair growth  
hair colour changes  
accumulation of fluid between the layers of tissue that line the lungs and chest cavity  
breast tenderness  
chest pain (angina)  
swelling of the ankles, feet or fingers associated with or independent of weight gain  
reduced kidney function resulting in increased creatinine and urea levels in your blood  
extensive peeling and detachment of the skin on much of the body surface (toxic epidermal necrolysis)  
accumulation of fluid around the heart  
compression of the heart due to a build-up of fluid  
fluid retention  
gastrointestinal disorder  
increased hair growth on the face or body  
headache  
dizziness  
ankle swelling  
palpitations 
decrease in white blood cells  
reduction in blood platelets, which increases the risk of bleeding or bruising  
serious illness with blistering of the skin, mouth, eyes and genitals (Stevens-Johnson syndrome)

If any side effect becomes serious, or you notice one that is not listed here, tell your doctor or pharmacist. It may help to note down what happened, when it started, and how long it lasted. You can also report suspected side effects through the MHRA Yellow Card scheme. 
#### Does finasteride affect mental health?
Finasteride has been associated with depression, suicidal thoughts and sexual dysfunction.  
Depression may include feelings of low self-esteem, loss of interest in activities and feelings of hopelessness. Physical symptoms of depression may include headaches, tiredness and disturbed sleep patterns.  
Stop taking finasteride and contact a healthcare professional as soon as possible if you develop depression or suicidal thoughts. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. For urgent advice when there is no immediate danger, contact NHS 111 or your GP. 
#### 
Can finasteride affect fertility or sexual function? 
Yes. Finasteride can cause sexual side effects in some men, including reduced sex drive (libido), erectile dysfunction and problems with ejaculation.  
Some patients have reported sexual side effects that continued after treatment was stopped. The MHRA has also advised that sexual dysfunction may contribute to mood-related symptoms in some people.  
Finasteride has been associated with low mood, depression and suicidal thoughts. If you experience any changes in your mood, mental wellbeing or sexual function while taking finasteride, speak to your healthcare professional.  
Finasteride has also been associated with reduced semen quality and fertility problems in some men. Semen quality may improve after stopping finasteride, but the available evidence is limited and individual outcomes vary.

## Dual Action Tablets FAQs 
#### Warnings and when to avoid treatment 
Do not take this combination if:
you are allergic to any ingredient in either product  
you are female  
you have phaeochromocytoma  
you are already taking finasteride or dutasteride for a prostate condition (BPH)  
you have a history of heart disease, heart failure, angina, a previous heart attack, kidney disease, or blood pressure problems  
#### Stop treatment and contact a doctor if you experience  
signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the lips, face, tongue, or throat; difficulty swallowing; hives; breathing difficulties. Stop immediately and seek medical help.  
depression, or feelings of severe sadness or worthlessness  
changes in breast tissue, including lumps, pain, enlargement, or nipple discharge, which can be a sign of a serious condition  
light-headedness, dizziness or faintness, particularly on standing 
signs of fluid retention, including swelling of the ankles, feet or hands, tighter-fitting rings, or unexplained weight gain  
increasing breathlessness, wheeze, or unusual tiredness, especially with an existing heart condition  
new chest pain, or existing chest pain (angina) becoming more frequent or severe  
any other unexpected new symptom  
#### Can these treatments be used for all types of hair loss? 
No – this bundle is suitable for men who are experiencing [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/blogs/skin-and-hair-advice/male-pattern-baldness] male pattern baldness. Treatment is only available following a successful consultation and suitability is not guaranteed. If you are unsure about the cause of your hair loss, please speak to your doctor before requesting treatment.
#### Does this bundle work for women? 
The treatment used in the Dual Action bundle is not suitable for women.
#### Why am I shedding hair when I start treatment? 
Some people may notice increased shedding after starting treatment. This may be temporary, but contact the clinical team if it is severe, persistent or concerning.
