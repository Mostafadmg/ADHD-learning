## Why choose a hair loss treatment subscription?
Hair loss in men is really common – the most common form is male pattern baldness. It affects half of men aged 50 and as many as 30% of men aged 30.
Male pattern baldness is caused by cells in the scalp’s skin turning testosterone to a hormone called dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT affects the hair follicles, causing them to shrink and eventually stop growing hair. To give you the best chance at slowing and even halting hair loss, you’ll need to start taking a treatment like Finasteride on a daily basis, and this is where we can help.
Our [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/collections/hair-loss] hair loss service makes getting your hair loss treatment discreet and simple. And you could start to see an improvement in as little as three months. With our subscription service you don’t have to worry about remembering to reorder and keeping up with regular treatment. Not only will you be saving time, but you’ll be saving money as you’ll always get 15% off.
## When to start hair loss treatment
The treatment we offer should slow hair loss and for lots of men it will stimulate new growth too.
The bottom line is, it’s easier to prevent more hair being lost, than it is to regrow it. So, the sooner you start treatment after noticing thinning or balding, the better the results you should see.

You can have your hair loss treatment delivered to you or your [A:/pages/parcelshop-collection] nearest ParcelShop, at no extra cost. We deliver orders in plain packaging as standard, so only you'll know what's inside. You can see what [A:/pages/discreet-delivery] your order will look like here.

#### When will I pay for my hair loss treatment?
Your payment normally comes out of your account the day you request your treatment. After that your payment will come out every 4, 8, 12 or 24 weeks, depending on the subscription plan you’ve chosen.
If you start your subscription on the last day of the month (e.g. 31st October), your next payment will always be taken on the last day of the following month (e.g. 30th November).
#### How do I subscribe to a hair loss subscription plan?
Simply select Finasteride, and you’ll be guided through our online consultation. You’ll be asked a series of questions, to make sure the treatment is right for you.
If you don’t select a subscription at the start, you’ll be given the opportunity to subscribe and save later in the consultation.
#### Which hair loss treatments are available on subscription?
Currently we have [A:/products/finasteride-hair-loss] Finasteride available for 4, 8, 12 and 24-week subscriptions.
If you currently take [A:/products/propecia-hair-loss] Propecia for your hair loss, it contains the same active ingredient as Finasteride, so they both work in the same way to treat hair loss. Finasteride is the unbranded version of the treatment, making it a more cost-effective option. And if you choose to subscribe and save with our great value subscription plan, you’ll get 15% off too.
