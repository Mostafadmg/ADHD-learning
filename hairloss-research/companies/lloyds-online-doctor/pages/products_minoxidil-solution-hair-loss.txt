## What is minoxidil solution?
Minoxidil is one of the only proven treatments to tackle  [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/blogs/skin-and-hair-advice/male-pattern-baldness] male pattern baldness. It’s a solution that’s applied to the affected areas of the scalp to tackle the causes of hair loss. 
## How does minoxidil work?
Minoxidil is the name of the treatment and its active ingredient. It works by increasing the supply of blood and nutrients to the hair follicles, which in turn stimulates growth. By  [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/blogs/skin-and-hair-advice/hair-regrowth] stimulating growth, the process of hair loss slows down.
## How effective is minoxidil 5% solution?
Minoxidil 5% solution is effective for around  [A:https://www.jaad.org/article/S0190-9622(03)03692-2/fulltext] 9 in 10 men. This means that there’s around a 90% chance you’ll see some improvement in your hair loss within a year, while taking the treatment. 
As soon as you stop using the solution it will stop having an effect and it’s likely your hair loss will resume.
## How quickly will minoxidil start working?
The treatment itself gets to work as soon as you start using it, but you might not see any impact for between three and nine months.

## Minoxidil 5% solution FAQs
#### How to use Sons minoxidil
The solution comes in a small bottle with a pump. Use the pump to apply 1ml of the solution to your scalp each morning and night. 
The scalp must be dry before applying the solution. Once applied, you can style your hair as normal. 
#### Is the Sons minoxidil 5% solution suitable for everyone?
Minoxidil is for men aged 18+ only. It shouldn’t be used by men who:
Are allergic to any of the ingredients
Have [A:/blogs/wellbeing-and-lifestyle-advice/how-to-take-your-blood-pressure-at-home] high blood pressure
Have an infected, irritated or inflamed scalp
Have psoriasis or sunburn on the scalp
Have a dressing or bandage on the head
Have a fully shaved scalp
#### Can I use other hair loss treatments alongside minoxidil?
Yes, lots of men, if their clinically suitable, use [A:/products/finasteride-regaine-bundle-hair-loss] minoxidil alongside Finasteride, a prescription-only hair loss treatment.
You could also try DHT blocking shampoo and biotin. To find out more about these treatments, visit our [A:/products/finasteride-regaine-bundle-hair-loss] hair loss bundle page.
#### Potential side effects to minoxidil 5% solution
Like all medications, there’s always a chance you might experience side effects with minoxidil. These tend to be mild and not very common but are worth bearing in mind. Most common side effects include skin irritation, headaches and shortness of breath. 
#### Alternative hair loss treatments
There are a variety of hair loss treatments available, but minoxidil and finasteride are the only proven ingredients other than a hair transplant. Understandably, hair transplants aren’t a solution for everyone – they’re expensive and invasive.
Other options to help hair loss include wigs, hair pieces or makeup. Some [A:/blogs/skin-and-hair-advice/vitamins-for-hair-loss] vitamins and supplements claim to aid hair loss and healthy hair growth. While there is limited evidence to suggest these work, there’s usually no harm in trying, when taken in safe doses.
Another way to keep your body (including your hair) as healthy as possible is to eat a healthy diet and make sure you’re getting all the [A:/blogs/skin-and-hair-advice/essential-nutrients-for-your-hair] essential nutrients.
