 Hair loss tablets
## About this treatment 
Taken daily with or without food 
Currently available only to eligible adult men  
Suitability is assessed individually by a clinician 
## What are hair loss tablets? 
Our Hair Loss Tablet contains a medicine that may be prescribed following an assessment by one of our clinicians.  
A clinician will assess your hair loss, medical history and current medicines before discussing suitable options. These may include non-medicinal, topical or oral approaches. Completing the consultation does not guarantee that treatment will be prescribed. 
This medicine is licensed in the UK for another condition. Its use for hair loss is not included in its marketing authorisation. Your clinician will explain the evidence, possible benefits, risks and alternatives before deciding whether it is suitable for you.
## Who are hair loss tablets suitable for? 
This treatment may be suitable for men aged 18 to 65 years experiencing male pattern hair loss.
## Who are hair loss tablets not suitable for? 
Hair loss tablets are not suitable for patients who: 
Are over 65 years old  
Have a history of phaeochromocytoma  
Have acute porphyria  
Have any heart or cardiovascular condition  
Have high or low blood pressure  
Have kidney impairment, including kidney failure or require dialysis  
Have liver impairment  
Are allergic to hair loss tablets or any of the ingredients. Check the patient information leaflet supplied with your medicine 
Are taking other medications that lower blood pressure (unless advised by your clinician. Always check before starting)  
Our clinicians will review your medical history and current medications to determine whether this treatment is appropriate for you.
## How do hair loss tablets work? 
The active ingredient works by increasing blood flow to hair follicles.  
The medicine is thought to prolong the active (anagen) phase of the hair cycle, although its full mechanism of action has not been completely established.  
Individual responses may vary, and ongoing use may be recommended where appropriate following review by a clinician.
## How long does it take hair loss tablets to work? 
It may take several months to see a change as hair loss treatment is a gradual process. You will usually need to take the treatment continuously for at least 6 to 12 months before assessing whether it is working for you.  
Continued treatment is required to maintain any benefits. If treatment is stopped, hair loss will generally begin to return over time.
## How do you take hair loss tablets? 
Hair loss tablets should be taken at the same time each day, with or without food. 
If you miss a dose, take the next dose at the usual time. Do not double up doses. 
Hair loss treatment is usually a long-term journey (if effective and tolerated). During this time you should continue to take hair loss tablets in order for the treatment to work. 
## Hair loss tablet side effects 
Side effects of hair loss tablets can include: 
Increased body hair growth 
Fluid retention 
Dizziness 
Headaches 
Changes in heart rate 
Some side effects require urgent medical assessment. Follow the safety advice supplied with your medicine and contact the service if you are concerned.
## When to seek medical advice  
Stop taking the medication and seek medical advice urgently if you develop:  
Severe dizziness or fainting  
Rapid or irregular heartbeat  
Chest pain  
Shortness of breath, particularly when resting or lying down  
Sudden unexplained weight gain  
Swelling of the face, ankles, feet or legs that develops suddenly or becomes severe

## Hair loss tablet FAQs 
#### Can hair loss tablets be taken with other hair loss treatments? 
Tell the clinician about all medicines and hair-loss treatments you use, including tablets, scalp treatments and products obtained elsewhere. Some treatments should not be used together. Do not start, stop or combine treatments unless your clinician advises you to.
#### Why am I shedding more hair when I start treatment? 
Some people notice more hair shedding in the first few weeks or months. This can be alarming, but it is a recognised and usually temporary effect as older hairs are shed to make way for new growth. It does not necessarily mean treatment is not working, and not everyone experiences it.  
Contact your clinician if:  
Shedding is severe 
Shedding continues for an extended period 
You are concerned about the pattern of your hair loss changing 
#### How do I store hair loss tablets? 
Store according to the information provided with your medicine and keep out of the sight and reach of children.
