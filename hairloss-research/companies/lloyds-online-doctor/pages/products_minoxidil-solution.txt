## Topical Minoxidil 5% Solution - what is it and how does it work?
Minoxidil is a treatment for [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/uk/hair-loss/male-pattern-baldness] male pattern baldness. It’s a solution that’s applied to the affected areas of the scalp. 
Minoxidil is the name of the treatment and its active ingredient. The precise way topical minoxidil stimulates hair growth is not fully understood. It may affect the [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/blogs/skin-and-hair-advice/hair-regrowth] hair-growth cycle, increase microcirculation to the hair follicles and may increase the diameter of the hair shaft.
## How long does it take for Minoxidil 5% to work?
Individual results vary. In general, twice-daily treatment for 2 to 4 months is needed before an effect is seen. Continued use is needed to maintain the effect; after stopping, regrown hair may be lost over the following 3 to 4 months.
## How to use Sons Minoxidil 5% Cutaneous Solution
The solution comes in a small bottle with a pump. Use the pump to apply 1ml (around six sprays) of the solution to the affected areas in the crown region of your scalp twice daily – once in the morning, once in the evening. Leave at least 12 hours between uses.
Do not exceed these two 1ml applications, regardless of how much of your scalp has been affected.
The scalp must be dry before applying the solution. Once applied, wash your hands carefully and make sure you don’t touch the moist lining of your mouth, nose and eyes. In case of accidental contact with these areas, rinse with plenty of water.
You can style your hair as normal after using minoxidil but wait about 4 hours before you moisten your scalp. Otherwise you risk washing the minoxidil off of your hair.
Keep minoxidil solution out of reach of children and pets.
## Topical Minoxidil side effects
Like all medications, there’s always a chance you might experience side effects with minoxidil. The most common side effect is headaches, which may affect more than 1 in 10 people.
Contact a doctor immediately if you notice any of the following symptoms as you may need medical attention:
Chest pain
Swelling of face, lips or throat or tightness in the throat
Swollen hands or feet
Generalised redness of the skin
Persistent redness or irritation of the scalp
Generalised itching
Low blood pressure
Faster heartbeat
Weakness or dizziness
Sudden and unexplained weight gain
Other side effects include:
Common side effects (may affect up to 1 in 10 people)
Irritation of the scalp, i.e. burning, stinging, dryness, scaling, itching or infected / inflamed hair follicles

Swollen arms and legs
Inflammatory skin reaction
Shortness of breath, difficulty in breathing
Uncommon side effects (may affect up to 1 in 100 people)
Dizziness
Nausea

Unknown frequency
Depressed mood

If you experience any side effects – including ones not listed above - talk to your doctor or pharmacist. Side effects can also be reported through the [A:https://yellowcard.mhra.gov.uk/] MHRA Yellow Card Scheme. For more information about the side effects of minoxidil, read the [A:https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/files/pil.100306.pdf] patient information leaflet.
## Who is Sons Minoxidil 5% Cutaneous Solution suitable for?
Minoxidil is for men aged 18-65 only.  Minoxidil shouldn’t be used by men who have:  
Allergic reactions to any of the ingredients  
 [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/uk/lifestyle-advice/what-is-high-blood-pressure] High blood pressure [A:#_msocom_1] [RS1]  [A:#_msocom_2] [DD2]  (treated or untreated)
An infected, irritated or inflamed scalp  
Psoriasis or sunburn on the scalp  
A dressing or bandage on the head  
A fully shaved scalp
Sudden or uneven hair loss
Cardiovascular disease or heart-rhythm problems
Total baldness
For further information on Minoxidil, please check the [A:https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/files/pil.100306.pdf] patient information leaflet.
Can women use Minoxidil?
This product must not be used by women. Women seeking treatment for hair loss should ask a pharmacist or GP about suitable options.

## Minoxidil 5% solution FAQs
#### Is Minoxidil safe to use?
Minoxidil is not suitable for everyone and can cause side effects. Read the patient information leaflet before use and ask a pharmacist or doctor if you are unsure whether it is suitable for you.
#### Can I use other hair loss treatments alongside minoxidil?
Do not use other medicines on your scalp at the same time unless a pharmacist or prescriber advises you to. If you use another hair-loss treatment, check before combining them.
#### Alternative hair loss treatments
Other options to help hair loss include wigs, hair pieces, makeup or a hair transplant. Understandably, hair transplants aren’t a solution for everyone – they’re expensive and invasive form of hair loss treatment. 
There is limited evidence that supplements help with hair loss unless a deficiency has been identified. Ask a healthcare professional before taking supplements.
Another way to keep your body (including your hair) as healthy as possible is to eat a healthy diet and make sure you’re getting all the  [A:https://onlinedoctor.lloydspharmacy.com/uk/hair-loss-advice/essential-nutrients-for-your-hair] essential nutrients.
