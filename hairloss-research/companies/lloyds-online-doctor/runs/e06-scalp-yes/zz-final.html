Consultation overview:

  • Your hair loss is consistent with male pattern hair loss

Our recommendation:

  • You're suitable for Finasteride tablets and Minoxidil tablets
  • As you're new to hair loss treatment, we'd recommend starting with a licensed treatment, such as Finasteride tablets
  • Combining Finasteride with Minoxidil may provide greater benefits than using either treatment alone, as they work in different ways.
Combination Treatments
Our most effective hair loss treament. Combine the benefits of two proven hair loss treatments.
Finasteride forms the foundation of treatment by helping to protect hair follicles from DHT, the hormone responsible for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil complements this by stimulating growth and increasing hair density. Together, they tackle both hair loss and regrowth for a more complete approach to treatment.
