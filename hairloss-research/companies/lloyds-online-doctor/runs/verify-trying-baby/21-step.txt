Finasteride and trying for a baby

Finasteride can affect sperm quality in some men, which may make it harder to conceive. This is uncommon and sperm quality usually improves after stopping treatment.

Only small amounts of finasteride pass into semen. Harm to a baby through this exposure is considered very unlikely.

As a precaution, we don't prescribe finasteride to men who are trying for a baby.

If you are planning to try for a baby, we recommend stopping finasteride at least 3 months before trying to conceive.
