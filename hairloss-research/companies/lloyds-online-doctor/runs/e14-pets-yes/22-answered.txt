Living with Pets

You've told us that you live with pets. Some of our treatments contain topical minoxidil, which is toxic to all pets including cats and dogs.

The main risk is accidental exposure, for example if a pet licks minoxidil from your fingers, hair, skin, clothing, or bedding after application.

Oral tablets or capsules are less likely to pose a risk because the medication does not sit on your hair or skin in the same way. However, tablets or capsules are still harmful if a pet ingests them.

By continuing, you confirm that you understand this risk and will take appropriate precautions to protect your pets.
