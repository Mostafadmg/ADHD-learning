Your Safety
Important safety information for your treatment:

Finasteride has been associated with causing:

  • Low mood, depression, and suicidal thoughts
  • Sexual side effects such as reduced sex drive, difficulty getting or maintaining an erection, and problems with ejaculation.
  • Changes in breast tissue, including lumps, pain, or nipple discharge.

If you develop depression or suicidal thoughts, stop treatment and contact your doctor immediately. If you ever feel at risk of harming yourself or have seriously harmed yourself, call emergency services on 999 immediately.
