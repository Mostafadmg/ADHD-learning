  Q 107978 [MESSAGE] required=true title="What causes hair loss?"
    TEXT: Male pattern hair loss is caused by a combination of genetics and sensitivity to DHT (dihydrotestosterone) , a hormone made from testosterone. In people who are susceptible, DHT gradually causes affected hair follicles to shrink and shortens the hair growth phase. New hairs become finer and shorter and may eventually stop growing in some areas of the scalp. Treatments can help slow further hair loss and may support regrowth. Options include finasteride and minoxidil , which work in different ways.

  Q 107979 [MESSAGE] required=true title="How does finasteride work?"
