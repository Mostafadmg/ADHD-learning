How does minoxidil work?

Minoxidil increases blood flow and nutrients to hair roots.

Used regularly, it can help hair grow back thicker over time. It usually takes a few months to notice a difference.

Some men use finasteride and minoxidil together. Complete your assessment to find out what's right for you.
