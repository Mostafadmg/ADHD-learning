Medical history

Steroids are thought to create male pattern hair loss by causing your body to produce higher levels of dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT acts on the hair follicles on your scalp, causing them to weaken over time. They eventually die and stop growing back, which results in baldness.

Taking Finasteride or Propecia is unlikely to be helpful if you are still taking steroids.

Steroids can also cause lots of other problems such as erectile dysfunction, infertility, heart and kidney problems as well as mood disorders.
