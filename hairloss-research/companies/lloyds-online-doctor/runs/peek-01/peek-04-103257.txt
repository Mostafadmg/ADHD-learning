Treating extensive hair loss

Finasteride and minoxidil are generally most effective for early to moderate male pattern hair loss at the crown and top of the scalp.

If your hair loss is extensive, treatment may still help slow further hair loss, but significant regrowth is less likely. Results vary between individuals.
