How effective is Minoxidil 5% Spray? 
Clinical studies have shown that many men experience improvement in their hair loss while using minoxidil consistently. In one study, 71% showed improved hair density over 16 weeks and in another study, 84% of patients found the treatment effective over 52 weeks. Individual results vary, and not everyone will respond to treatment. 
 How long does it take to work? 
The treatment begins working when you start using it, but visible results may not be noticed for between 3 and 9 months. 
You will usually need to use Minoxidil continuously for at least 6 to 12 months before assessing whether it is working for you. Continued treatment is required to maintain any benefits. If treatment is stopped, hair loss will generally begin to return over time. 
 Can I use Minoxidil with Finasteride?

How effective is finasteride and oral minoxidil together? 
Finasteride and minoxidil work in different ways and may be used together when clinically appropriate. Because oral minoxidil is used off-label for hair loss, the evidence for this specific combination is more limited than for finasteride alone, or for finasteride combined with topical minoxidil. Individual results vary, and not everyone responds to treatment. Your clinician can talk you through the reasoning behind recommending this combination for you specifically. 
 Does oral minoxidil work as well as topical minoxidil? 
Yes. Research so far suggests it can work about as well. 
Several studies have compared oral minoxidil with topical minoxidil. Three studies published between 2024 and 2025 found that both treatments improved hair thickness and hair growth by a similar amount. 
One of these studies combined the results from multiple clinical trials and found no meaningful difference between oral and topical minoxidil at the doses tested. This research is still relatively new, and treatment responses vary between individuals. Speak to your clinician if you have questions about what this means for you. 
 How long does it take to work? 
The treatment begins working when you start taking it, but visible results may not be noticed for several months. You will usually need to take finasteride and oral minoxidil continuously for at least 6 to 12 months before assessing whether it is working for you. Continued treatment is required to maintain any benefits. If treatment is stopped, hair loss will generally begin to return over time. 
 Why is oral minoxidil prescribed off-label for hair loss? 
Although oral minoxidil is licensed for severe hypertension, clinicians may prescribe it off-label for hair loss at a much lower dose than normally used for high blood pressure when they believe it is appropriate and in the patient's best interests. 
 Why am I shedding more hair when I start treatment? 
Some people notice more hair shedding in the first few weeks or months of starting minoxidil. This can be alarming, but it is a recognised and usually temporary effect as older hairs are shed to make way for new growth. It does not necessarily mean treatment is not working, and not everyone experiences it. 
Contact your clinician if shedding is severe, continues for an extended period, or you are concerned about the pattern of your hair loss changing. 
 Will oral minoxidil lower my blood pressure? 
Minoxidil lowers blood pressure by relaxing blood vessels. The dose used for hair loss is much lower than the dose used to treat hypertension, and evidence to date suggests most people with normal blood pressure do not experience a clinically significant drop at this lower dose. However, the evidence base is smaller and less mature than for the licensed hypertension use, and individual responses vary.
